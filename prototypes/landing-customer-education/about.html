Differentiators

What makes ClassroomIO different

Open source↗

Open source and self-hostable

ClassroomIO is AGPL licensed and free to self-host, whereas Skilljar, LearnUpon and Intellum are closed-source platforms you can only rent. You can read the code, run it on your own infrastructure and leave without losing your data.

Pricing↗

Published pricing from $39 a month

Starter is $39 a month and Growth is $99 a month, with every plan listed on our pricing page. Skilljar, LearnUpon and Intellum use quote-based pricing, so you cannot compare costs before a sales call.

Fees↗

0% transaction fees and unlimited courses

We charge no transaction fees and cap neither courses nor video streaming on any plan. Pricing scales with monthly active learners only, so growing your catalog never raises your bill.

Focus↗

Built for customer education, not generic training

ClassroomIO focuses on teaching customers how to use your product, with CRM sync, support desk integrations and branded academies built in.

AI and API↗

AI-native and developer-first

AI course creation, an AI tutor, a REST API and webhooks are available from the Starter plan. Many competitors reserve API access and automation for their top enterprise tiers.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Got questions? Here is everything you need to know about getting started.

What counts as a learner?

Learners are counted as monthly active learners, not registered ones. People you bulk-invite who never log in do not count toward your plan.

Are there transaction fees?

No. ClassroomIO charges 0% transaction fees on every plan.

Is video streaming really unlimited?

Yes. Unlimited video streaming is included on every plan. File storage has an included allowance with a hard limit and a paid add-on if you need more.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You can try Growth free for 14 days, no card required. You can also self-host the open source Community edition for free.

What does annual billing save?

Annual billing gives you two months free, about 17 percent off the monthly price.

Can I self-host ClassroomIO?

Yes. Community is free under AGPL. If you want to keep modifications private and get support, the Self-hosted License is $2,990 per year per organization.
