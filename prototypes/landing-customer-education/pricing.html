FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Got questions? Here is everything you need to know about getting started.

What counts as a learner?

Learners are counted as monthly active learners, not registered ones. People you bulk-invite who never log in do not count toward your plan.

Are there transaction fees?

No. ClassroomIO charges 0% transaction fees on every plan.

Is video streaming really unlimited?

Yes. Unlimited video streaming is included on every plan. File storage has an included allowance with a hard limit and a paid add-on if you need more.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You can try Growth free for 14 days, no card required. You can also self-host the open source Community edition for free.

What does annual billing save?

Annual billing gives you two months free, about 17 percent off the monthly price.

Can I self-host ClassroomIO?

Yes. Community is free under AGPL. If you want to keep modifications private and get support, the Self-hosted License is $2,990 per year per organization.
