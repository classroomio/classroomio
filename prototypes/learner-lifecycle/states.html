8 · Delete is gated behind archive

Permanent delete is disabled until every selected learner is already archived. The consequence lines under each action are what steer someone to the reversible option.

9 · Delete confirmation — type to confirm

Type-to-confirm is delete only. Adding friction to reversible actions just trains people to type past warnings.

Permanently delete 3,214 learners?

10 · Export menu — scoped and counted

Never ambiguous what is being downloaded. PDF disables above 2,000 rows and says why, because a 20,000-row PDF helps nobody.

11 · Undo, because archive is reversible

Archived rows leave the default view. The toast names how many, so their disappearance is explained rather than startling. Delete gets no undo — and the dialog said so.

12 · Mobile — the filter popover becomes a sheet
