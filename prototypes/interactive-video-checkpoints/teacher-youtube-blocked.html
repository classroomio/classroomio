In-video questions need an uploaded video

YouTube, Google Drive, and pasted embed links play in an iframe. ClassroomIO cannot pause them at a timestamp or lock seeking, so questions would never fire — the same trap as a builder that offers “resume video” when the player has no such option.

Upload the clip (MP4) on this tab. Then you can drop questions on the timeline, the same way Watch to complete already works.
