Interactive Video Checkpoints

Pause an uploaded lesson video, ask a single-choice or true/false question on the player, then resume. The six-video course, graded exam, and certificate stay the current ClassroomIO model. Engineering spec: prd/interactive-video-checkpoints/README.md.

1 · Teacher — authoring
Course · ContentsSix lessons + final examNo new content type. Question-count chips on lessons that have checkpoints.
Lesson · Video tabTimeline on an uploaded videoMarkers on the scrubber, list under the player, Add question at the playhead.
DialogAdd a questionSingle answer or true/false, timestamp, resume any vs require correct.
YouTube embedBlocked stateNo fake timeline. Upload a video if you want in-player questions.
2 · Learner — pause, answer, resume
LMS · LessonClick-through playerPlay, hit a marker, answer, resume. This is the working end-to-end demo.
OverlayQuestion on the videoPaused at 0:45. Overlay lives on the player, not a new page.
After answerVideo continuesSame timestamp. Marker is spent. Next lesson stays locked until this one completes.
3 · Exam, certificate, public
LMS · ExerciseGraded final examUnchanged Exercise page. This is what the certificate actually scores.
LMS · CertificateAuto-issued on passProgress 100% + exam ≥ 70%. In-video answers are not on this document.
Org site · PublicIn-memory practiceOverlays can run; answers are not saved. Refresh clears them.
Decisions baked into these prototypes:
· Checkpoints are on the lesson video, never extra rows in the contents tree.
· Only uploaded videos get a timeline. YouTube is an honest blocked state.
· Default resume is any answer; require-correct is opt-in per question.
· The overlay has an explicit Continue after selecting an option.
· The final exam Exercise is still what issues the certificate.
