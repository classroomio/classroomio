Interactive Video Checkpoints

Pause an uploaded lesson video, ask any auto-gradable question on the player, then resume. The six-video course, graded exam, and certificate stay the current ClassroomIO model. Engineering spec: prd/interactive-video-checkpoints/README.md.

1 · Teacher — lesson canvas (block editor)
Course · ContentsSix lessons + final examNo new content type. Question-count chips on lessons that have questions.
Edit mode · Video blockQuestions live in the blockMarker dots on the scrubber, a compact strip under the player with Results and Add question, and the block menu. Same canvas as the lesson editor redesign.
DrawerAdd a questionRight-side drawer, not a modal. Any auto-gradable type, time, resume any vs require correct.
DrawerResultsPer-question answered, % correct and common wrong answer; click a row for the learner list; Export CSV.
Preview modeLearner experienceBlocks without editor chrome. Questions pause the video, but Preview answers are not recorded.
Free planUpgrade stateTimeline stays visible; Add question and Results are locked behind the upgrade prompt.
YouTube blockUpload to add questionsNo fake timeline on an embed. The block offers “Upload a video instead”.
2 · Learner — pause, answer, resume
LMS · LessonClick-through playerPlay, hit a marker, get it wrong and retry, try to skip ahead, finish and complete the lesson.
OverlayQuestion types on the playerSwitch between every auto-gradable type as it appears in the overlay card.
3 · Exam, certificate, public
LMS · ExerciseGraded final examUnchanged Exercise page. This is what the certificate actually scores.
LMS · CertificateAuto-issued on passProgress 100% + exam ≥ 70%. In-video answers are not on this document.
Org site · PublicIn-memory practiceOverlays can run; answers are not saved. Refresh clears them.
Decisions baked into these prototypes:
· Questions are part of a Video block on the lesson canvas, never extra rows in the contents tree.
· Adding and editing, and Results, open as right-side drawers, not modals.
· Authoring and Results are plan-gated with video upload; learners always see existing questions.
· Preview answers are never recorded or counted.
· Teachers can add every auto-gradable question type (premium types stay plan-gated). Manual types stay on the exam.
· Only uploaded videos get questions. YouTube, Vimeo and Drive show an upload prompt.
· Default resume is any answer; require-correct is opt-in per question.
· The overlay has an explicit Continue after selecting an option.
· The final exam Exercise is still what issues the certificate.
