Prototype. "Try an answer" and every grade in this prototype come from the real math engine (Compute Engine) running in your browser. Photo, Draw and Ask AI return sample results; the real version sends the image or prompt to a vision model. Settings you change here carry over to tabs 2 and 3.

What the student sees

  • Each question picks its own keypad. Question 1 is algebra; question 2 is numbers and fractions only.
  • Answers are real math, not text: type 2(x+1) or 2+2x and the engine decides.
  • The hint button is the AI tutor in hint-only mode. It checks the student's answer with the engine first, so it never "agrees" with a wrong answer.
  • Question 4 grades the final answer at once and sends the working (typed, photo or drawing) to the teacher with an AI-suggested mark.
  • When you submit, your answers appear as the "You" row in tab 3.
