ClassroomIO · Prototype + PRD handoff

Mobile App

A learner-first iOS and Android app for the people companies actually train — frontline staff, field technicians, and distributed employees who often have no work laptop and unreliable connectivity. It opens on what needs your attention, works offline, and finally gives ClassroomIO a push channel for deadlines and renewals. A small tutor mode handles grading and attendance on the go; authoring and org administration stay on the web.

Built on the real packages/ui tokens, rendered in a 390 × 844 device frame. Every page has a theme toggle and cross-links so you can click through each journey end to end. The engineering PRD lives at prd/mobile-app/README.md.

Explicitly not in the app. Course, lesson, and exercise authoring; the media manager and AI course builder; org settings, team management, billing, SSO configuration, custom domains, and API keys; live-class video hosting; SCORM playback; and any purchase flow at all — no checkout, no plan upgrade, no AI token packs, on any storefront.

Hard dependency. Push notifications ride on prd/notification-system. Mobile contributes a device-token registry and an Expo Push transport in the existing apps/jobs worker — never a second notification pipeline.

Offline scope. v1 caches content and queues two monotonic writes (lesson completion, playback position). Exercises, submissions, and grading stay online so no assessment record can be corrupted by a replay — the same line Canvas and Blackboard draw.
