        1. 01

          Spot the problem

          Your summary cards show progress, scores, overdue work and completions for a course, a cohort or your whole academy. When more than one in twenty learners fall behind, the Overdue card turns red, so you notice without digging.

        2. 02

          See exactly who

          Click the number and a list opens with the learners behind it. You see why each one was flagged: no login for two days, a score under 50%, or work that's past its due date.

        3. 03

          Give them a nudge

          Email one learner a reminder, or remind everyone with an overdue goal in one click. If someone already had a reminder today, ClassroomIO skips them, so nobody gets spammed.
