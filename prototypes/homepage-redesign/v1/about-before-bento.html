Helping teams teach their customers.

ClassroomIO is the open source customer education platform. We build it in the open, for companies that want customers to learn their product and stick around.

Open source · AGPL-3.0 Cloud or self-hosted Lessons completed This week

What drives everything we build.

Two ideas sit behind every release: customers should be able to learn the products they pay for, and the software that teaches them should be open.

Our mission Help software companies teach customers their product, so they get value sooner and stay longer.

Our vision Every product comes with a place to learn it, on software anyone can inspect, extend and run.

Open by default ClassroomIO is AGPL-3.0 and on GitHub. Run it on our cloud or on your own servers.

Your brand first Your academy carries your name, your colours and your domain, not ours.

Our story

How an open source learning platform became a customer education platform.

We set out to build the simplest learning platform we could. Our customers showed us where it mattered most.

ClassroomIO began as an open source learning platform. Early on, people used it to run tech boot camps and training classes for their clients. From day one the goal was a platform that’s powerful, customisable and affordable, and that puts your brand first.

Over time, the teams getting the most from it were software companies teaching their own customers. So that’s who we build for now: an open source customer education platform you can run on our cloud or on your own servers.
