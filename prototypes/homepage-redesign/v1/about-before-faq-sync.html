FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Got questions? Here is what people ask us most.

What is ClassroomIO?

ClassroomIO is an open source customer education platform. You create courses, deliver them in a branded academy and track how learners progress.

Is ClassroomIO really open source?

Yes. The code is on GitHub under the AGPL-3.0 licence, so you can inspect it, extend it and run it yourself.

How does ClassroomIO pricing work?

You pay for monthly active learners. Starter is $39 a month for 200, Growth $99 for 1,000 and Scale $299 for 5,000. Above 5,000, Enterprise is priced by learner band. Yearly billing gives you two months free. See pricing

Can I try ClassroomIO before I buy it?

Yes. You can try Growth free for 14 days, and you don’t need a card to start.

Can I run ClassroomIO on my own servers?

Yes. The Community edition is free under AGPL-3.0. The Self-hosted License adds a commercial licence, email support and upgrade help for $2,990 a year per organisation.

How do I get help?

Book a call with the team, ask the community on Discord, or open an issue on GitHub. Enterprise plans add an SLA, priority support and a named contact.
