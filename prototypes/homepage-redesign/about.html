Our story

Education should work like software engineering: from anywhere, and still successful.

In 2020, everyone was confused. Schools, teachers and companies had to teach without a room, and almost nobody was ready.

Most training still needs a person, in a room or on a call, every time. That ties good education to where you are and what you can afford.

We’re building the tools that let anyone take any form of education, anywhere, anytime. We want to be to education what WordPress became to publishing: the platform anyone can build their teaching on.

Frequently asked questions

Got questions? Here is everything you need to know about getting started.

What is ClassroomIO?

ClassroomIO is a customer education platform that helps SaaS companies grow by teaching their customers. You create courses with AI, deliver them on your own domain and track how learners progress.

How does ClassroomIO pricing work?

There is a free plan to get started. Starter is $39 a month for 200 active learners, Growth is $99 for 1,000 and Scale is $299 for 5,000, with two months free when you pay yearly. Enterprise is priced by quote and includes SSO.

Can I use my own domain and branding?

Yes. A custom domain, your logo and your colours are included on every paid plan, so they are not add-ons.

Can ClassroomIO connect to the tools I already use?

Yes. Zapier, webhooks, the public API and the MCP server let you move courses and learner data between ClassroomIO and your other tools. There are no native Salesforce or Gainsight connectors.

Can I move my courses from another platform?

Yes. Enterprise plans include free migration from any LMS. On any plan you can bring in your videos, slides and documents, or create courses through the API.

Can I run ClassroomIO on my own servers?

Yes. There is a self-hosting guide and the code is open source. Some Enterprise features, such as SSO, need a licence key when you self-host.
