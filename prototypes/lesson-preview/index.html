Lesson preview

Let an unenrolled visitor watch or read selected lessons before they enroll or pay. The hero of the public course page plays a preview lesson; a card under the player lists the others; the curriculum marks which rows are free to open. Explore uses the same player in a wider modal. Teachers decide which lessons are previews and which one is the hero.

Interactive HTML. Toggle language and free/paid on the landing page. Compare a wide Explore modal (has previews) with today’s narrow one (no previews). Engineering notes live in prd/lesson-preview/README.md.
