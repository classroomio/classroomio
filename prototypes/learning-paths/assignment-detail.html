Build your first customer workflow

Completed

Instructions

Using the workflow builder, create an automation that welcomes a new customer and routes them to the right onboarding track based on their plan.

What you'll submit

  • A working workflow with at least one trigger, one condition, and two actions
  • A short write-up (150–250 words) explaining your trigger and condition choices
  • A screenshot of the workflow running in test mode

Grading criteria

  • Workflow triggers and completes without errors
  • Condition correctly branches by plan type
  • Write-up clearly explains the reasoning behind each step
