Learner account menu on the academy landing page

A signed-in learner arriving at an academy's front door should be able to see who they are signed in as, reach their courses and certificates, set their theme, and sign out — without entering the LMS first.

Decisions these screens encode

  1. CTA button + avatar — the avatar does not replace the nav button.
  2. The CTA repeats at the bottom of the card, filled and full width.
  3. Continue Learning goes to /lms — no resume-last-lesson, no progress fetch in v1.
  4. Items: My Courses, My Certificates, Account Settings, Theme, Log Out.
  5. Non-members keep the avatar and get Join Academy, subject to existing invite-only gating.
  6. Staff see what learners see — no Dashboard or edit shortcuts.
  7. Theme sets the app/LMS preference, not this page. Landing themes are fixed palettes with no dark variant.
  8. One shared component dropped into all 11 theme navs.
