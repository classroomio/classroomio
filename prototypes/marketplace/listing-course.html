Customer Onboarding Academy

A ready-to-brand onboarding course that takes new customers from sign-up to their first real win in under an hour.

About this template

We built this for our own SaaS customers and rebuilt it three times based on completion data. It opens with a two-minute "why you're here" lesson, then walks through account setup, the core workflow and inviting teammates, with a short knowledge check after each section. Swap in your product screenshots and help-center links and it's ready to publish.

Lessons are short (4–7 minutes), written in plain language, and each ends with one concrete action. The final exercise asks learners to complete a real task in your product and paste a link as proof.
