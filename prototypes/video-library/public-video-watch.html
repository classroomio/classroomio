What's new in Loopwise 4.2 — smart segments & saved views

14 March 2026 · 1,204 views · 4 min watch Release notesAdmin

Smart segments let you define an audience once and reuse it everywhere — reports, exports, and automations. Saved views do the same thing for layout: build a table the way you like it, share it with your team, pin it to the sidebar. This walkthrough covers both, plus the new bulk editor and the changes to permissions that come with them.
