What the prototype is asserting. The nav entry reads "Product tours", not "Videos" — the label is org-editable copy from landingpage.videos.navLabel, and the system never writes into landingpage.navItems. The first two cards are pinned; the rest are auto-filled newest-first from the Release notes and Getting started tags. Every colour here comes from a --landing-* variable, which is what lets one videos.svelte serve all 11 themes. If the section resolves to zero videos, both the section and the nav entry disappear.
