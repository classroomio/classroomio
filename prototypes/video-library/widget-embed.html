Single sign-on

Loopwise supports SAML SSO with Okta, Google Workspace and Auth0. Configure your provider first, then map groups to Loopwise roles.

POST /v1/sso/config
{
  "provider": "okta",
  "metadata_url": "https://acme.okta.com/app/.../sso/saml/metadata"
}

Group mapping is evaluated on every login, so removing someone from a group revokes their access at their next sign-in.
