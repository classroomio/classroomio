Verifiable Certificates

Every certificate on a paid plan carries a verification code and QR code. A third party scans or types it, lands on a public page on the customer's own brand, and sees whether the certificate is valid, expired or revoked. Course staff can set a validity period and revoke from the dashboard. The engineering PRD lives at prd/verifiable-certificates/README.md.

Teacher sets validity and fields→ Learner earns certificate→ Employer scans QR→ Public page: valid / expired / revoked→ Teacher revokes
