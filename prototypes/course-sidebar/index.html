Course Sidebar Navigation Prototypes

Three HTML mockups for helping students find the next lesson without scrolling past completed items.

Prototype 1

Continue / Jump to Next

Sticky “Resume lesson” card that scrolls to the next incomplete item in the full outline.
Prototype 2

Collapse Completed

Hide completed items by default; show a compact summary that can be expanded.
Prototype 3

Up Next Panel

Focused “Up next” tab with current item + short queue; full outline on a second tab.
