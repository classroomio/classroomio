Which one should you pick?

Thought Industries pairs learning with a knowledge layer. ClassroomIO is the focused academy.

Choose Thought Industries if

  • You want a governed knowledge layer and AI answers alongside your courses.
  • You need content recommendations or monetization.
  • You want a headless front end for your academy.

Choose ClassroomIO if

  • You want a published price and a free start, not a demo-priced Academy.
  • You want up to 10,000 students on one plan, not tier caps of 500, 2,000 or 5,000.
  • You want a narrower product that is quick to set up.
