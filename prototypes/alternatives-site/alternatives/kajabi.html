Which one should you pick?

Kajabi is a creator business suite. ClassroomIO is a customer academy.

Choose Kajabi if

  • You sell and market digital products with funnels, email and landing pages.
  • You want communities or a branded mobile app.
  • You want selling, marketing and course hosting in one suite.

Choose ClassroomIO if

  • You want unlimited courses and collaborators, not product caps of 5 or 50 and 2, 11 or 26 admin users.
  • You want to start free and pay $35 a month, not $179.
  • You want AI course creation and MCP access on every plan, including the free one.
