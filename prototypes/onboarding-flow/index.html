ClassroomIO · Prototype

Onboarding, end to end

A new customer-education lead signs up, tells ClassroomIO about their business, and lands on a Courses page that is already branded and already has starter courses. The flow ends on that landing page, as visitors would see it, with a short list of what to do next.

Built on the ClassroomIO brand system: one blue (#0233BD) on warm paper and sand, Geist and Geist Mono, the notch block as the signature shape, and the system's own Machine illustrations and block loader. Earlier explorations are in the design options.

Stage 1Create your account with a Machine illustration and a sand testimonial card
Stage 2Verify your email by link, not a code, as the app does today
Stage 3Your business an accordion that opens each section for you (outcome, company, team, how customers learn today, then an optional "How did you hear about us?"), with a workspace block stack that grows beside it
PrepareYour drafted courses and path drop in as blocks simulated, and labelled as such
Stage 4Customise your portal a docked strip of the real eleven themes and a brand colour pill; paid themes open the pricing card
ThenOne dismissible integrations modal (MCP, Public API, Webhooks, HubSpot), then the academy's own landing page with a Next steps panel

What is real and what is not

Theme names, descriptions, preview images and the free/paid split are the real picker's (landingpage.svelte, FREE_LANDING_PAGE_THEMES). The five-plan pricing is the product owner's proposal (README.md § Pricing), not what ships today. Integrations show what the dashboard actually ships: only MCP and the Public API exist. No website is fetched, no model is called, no checkout runs and no credentials are collected.
