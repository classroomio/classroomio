Bring SCORM packages into ClassroomIO

Clickable prototypes for every SCORM surface in prd/scorm-support/v2. They use the app’s real tokens, course shell and copy, and the seeded Coursera Test organization’s compliance course, HIPAA Awareness 2026. Each page has a state bar at the bottom left. SCORM is a separate activity content type with its own routes and pages, and a SCORM course format holds exactly one package. Where the PRD and a prototype disagree, the PRD wins.

Calls made in the prototypes

For the reviewer to confirm or veto
  • SCORM items get four tabs (Package, Instructions, Results, Settings) instead of a fifth material tab, on their own activity page.
  • Results live on the item, not on the course Analytics page.
  • Students see a status pill and a save indicator under the package. Neither is in the shipped lesson page today.
  • An xAPI or cmi5 ZIP gets its own error, so authors know to re-export as SCORM.
  • A package with several modules stays one item. The module menu is a row of chips above the player, not extra rows in the course sidebar.
  • A retake starts from a “Start a new attempt” button in the review banner, never automatically, so students can still review after finishing.
  • Several courses are created at once from the Media Manager’s SCORM filter, behind a Select button.
  • In a SCORM course the Package nav item sits where Content sits today and behaves like every other nav item: click to enter, nothing to expand.
