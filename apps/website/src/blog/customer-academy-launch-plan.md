This is a 30-day plan for launching a first customer academy with one course. Copy it into your own doc and change the names. If you want the background first, read [what a customer academy is](/blog/what-is-a-customer-academy).

## Before you start

Write down two things in one sentence each.

- The audience: for example, new customers on the Team plan.
- The outcome: for example, a customer can set up their first integration without contacting support.

Everything below serves that sentence. If a lesson does not help, cut it.

## Week 1: decide and collect

1. Pick the audience and outcome above.
2. List the 10 support questions new customers ask most. Your support inbox or help desk search report is the best source.
3. Gather existing material: help articles, onboarding call notes, webinar recordings, release notes.
4. Choose where the academy will live. Decide on a subdomain such as learn.yourcompany.com.
5. Pick one owner. A single person who can say yes to decisions gets this done faster than a committee.

## Week 2: outline and draft

1. Group the 10 questions into 4 to 6 lessons, ordered by what the customer needs first.
2. Fill in the course outline below.
3. Draft each lesson from the material you collected. AI drafting can speed up the first pass, but someone who knows the product should review every lesson before it is published.
4. Write one exercise for the end of the course that checks the outcome from your sentence.

## Week 3: build and test

1. Set up the academy: your domain, logo, colors and a catalog page.
2. Add the course and the exercise. Add a certificate if the course ends in a skill worth showing.
3. Ask three customers, or three colleagues who are not on the product team, to take the course. Watch where they hesitate.
4. Fix the confusing lessons. Cut anything nobody needed.

## Week 4: launch and measure

1. Link the academy from your onboarding emails, the in-app help menu and your docs.
2. Tell your customer success team what the course covers so they can send it to customers who ask the matching question.
3. Announce it to existing customers with one email that states the outcome, not the feature list.
4. Set a calendar reminder for day 45 to review the numbers below.

### Metrics to track

| Metric | What it tells you | When to look |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enrolled customers | Whether people find the academy | Weekly |
| Completion rate | Whether the course works | Weekly |
| Where learners stop | Which lesson to fix first | After 20 learners |
| Support tickets on covered topics | Whether the academy answers questions | Monthly |
| Time to first result | Whether onboarding is faster | Monthly |

## After day 30

Fix the lesson where most learners stop. Then add a second course on the next most common support topic. Do not add more courses until the first one has a completion rate you are happy with.
