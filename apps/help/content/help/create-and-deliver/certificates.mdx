---
title: Create Certificates
description: Configure certificate rules for a course, set completion requirements, and let students download their certificates.
---

Certificates recognize students for completing a course. You configure the rules on a per-course basis — what students must complete and when certificates expire. Students can then download their certificate from the **Certificates** tab in the LMS.

:::warning[Paid plan required]
Certificate features require a paid plan. The controls are visible on free plans but disabled.
:::

## Configure certificate rules

Certificate options live in two places in your course. Download and the email message sit on the **Certificates**
tab, under its **Settings** sub-tab. The rules that decide who qualifies sit in the **Completion rules** section of the
course's own **Settings** tab. **Certificates > Settings** shows a summary of those rules with a button that takes you
straight to them.

:::info
Both places are called Settings. **Certificates > Settings** is a sub-tab inside Certificates. The course **Settings**
tab is a separate item in the course menu. The table below says which one each option is on.
:::

| What you want to set                | Where                                      |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Allow students download certificate | **Certificates > Settings**                |
| Email Message                       | **Certificates > Settings**                |
| Student progression                 | Course **Settings** > **Completion rules** |
| Course Completion Deadline          | Course **Settings** > **Completion rules** |

### On the Certificates tab

Open your course in the admin dashboard and go to the **Certificates** tab. It opens on the **Design** sub-tab, so
click **Settings** next to it.

![Certificates tab with the Design and Settings sub-tabs, showing the download toggle and the Completion rules summary with its Edit completion rules button](/help/certificates-rules.webp)

#### 1. Let students download their certificate

Under **Rules**, turn on **Allow students download certificate**. Students who qualify still complete the course and
earn their certificate record when this is off, but they cannot download the certificate itself.

#### 2. Check the completion rules

Under **Completion rules**, a short summary lists student progression and the deadline the course currently uses. Click
**Edit completion rules** to open the course **Settings** tab with the rules highlighted. On a free plan the button is
disabled.

#### 3. Add a custom email message (optional)

Enter text under **Email Message**. This message is included in the notification email sent to students when they earn their certificate.

### On the course Settings tab

Click **Edit completion rules** on **Certificates > Settings**, or open the course's **Settings** tab and scroll to
the **Completion rules** section. When you arrive from the Certificates tab, **Back to certificate settings** at the top
of the section takes you back.

![Course Settings page showing the Completion rules section with student progression and the deadline field](/help/certificates-completion-rule.webp)

Certificates always require every lesson and exercise in the course to be complete, so there is no completion percentage to set.

#### 1. Choose student progression

Pick **Free** or **Sequential** under **Student progression**. Sequential progression makes students finish each item
before the next one unlocks. Either mode still requires every item to be complete before the certificate is issued.

#### 2. Require a passing score on an exercise (optional)

Every exercise counts toward completion. To require a minimum score on one, open that exercise's settings, set its
completion rule to **Passed**, then choose the passing score. The exercise stays incomplete until the student submits an
attempt that reaches that score. The student's best attempt counts.

#### 3. Set a deadline (optional)

Enter a date and time in **Course Completion Deadline**. Students must complete every lesson and exercise at or before this datetime, in local time. A student who finishes exactly on the deadline still qualifies. Leave it empty for no deadline.

## Customize the design and add signatures

Set up how the certificate looks before students download it. On the **Certificates** tab, open the **Design** sub-tab and click **Customize design** to open the certificate editor.

![Certificate design editor showing the template picker and live preview](/help/certificates-design-editor.webp)

### 1. Pick a template and accent color

Choose a **Template** and an accent color to match your brand. A live preview shows the result as you change it.

### 2. Add signatories and signatures

Under **Signatories**, turn on **Show signatory 1** and fill in **Signatory 1 name** and **Signatory 1 role**, then
use **Signature image** to upload a signature graphic. You can enable a second signatory the same way.

![Signatories section with name, role, and signature image upload](/help/certificates-signatories.webp)

### 3. Set the certificate ID format (optional)

Set a **Certificate ID format** so every issued certificate carries a unique, verifiable reference.

:::info
Certificates are generated from the course's current design when downloaded. Later design and signature changes also affect new downloads for students who earned certificates earlier.
:::

## What students see

Once a student meets all the configured requirements, their certificate appears in the **Certificates** tab when they open the course in the LMS. They can view and download it from there.

![Student certificate view with download buttons](/help/certificates-student-download.webp)
