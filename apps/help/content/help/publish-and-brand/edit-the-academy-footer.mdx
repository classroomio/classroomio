The academy landing page footer appears below your public homepage content.

![Academy landing page Footer editor and preview](./images/edit-the-academy-footer/overview.webp)

1. Open **Distribute → Landing Page → Customize** and select **Footer**.
2. Edit the brand tagline and copyright text.
3. Add or update link columns and social links. Check each label and destination before saving.
4. Save, then inspect the footer on your live academy page.

Keep link text descriptive and point visitors to pages they can access without an admin account. See [Customize Your Academy Landing Page](/publish-and-brand/org-landing-page) for the rest of the page.
