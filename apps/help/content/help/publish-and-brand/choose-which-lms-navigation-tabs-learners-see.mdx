:::info[Turned-off pages also close by direct link]
Turning off Exercises or Community in **Settings → Customize LMS → Dashboard** hides the sidebar link and also closes those pages to anyone who opens them by a saved link or bookmark — they land on the Dashboard instead. The same applies to Certificates on the free plan. To send signed-in students somewhere specific at login, see [Send signed-in students straight to your learning portal](/publish-and-brand/student-home).
:::
