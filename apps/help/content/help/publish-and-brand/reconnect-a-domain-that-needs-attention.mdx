title: Reconnect a domain that needs attention
description: Restore a custom domain after a DNS or connection problem.
last_reviewed: '2026-10-21'
---

A domain marked **Reconnect required** or **Error** needs attention before you rely on it for academy links.

![Custom domain settings showing Reconnect required and the Reconnect domain action](./images/reconnect-a-domain-that-needs-attention/overview.webp)

1. Open **Settings → Domains** and read the current status and instructions.
2. Compare the displayed DNS records with your DNS provider and restore any missing or changed record.
3. Choose **Reconnect** for the existing hostname, then use **Refresh** to check its status.
4. When it shows **Verified**, open the HTTPS academy URL and test sign-in and a course link.

A domain pending DNS or verification may simply need more time after a record change. Do not share the custom URL as the main academy address until it works in a browser. See [Set Up a Custom Domain](/publish-and-brand/custom-domain).
