---
title: 'Understand MCP limits and permissions'
description: 'Check what a connected MCP client can access, who it acts as, and how request limits work.'
last_reviewed: '2026-10-30'
---

A client you connect by signing in acts as you in one workspace, with only the permissions you approved. ClassroomIO checks those permissions and your role on each request, and you must be an admin of the workspace. If you stop being an admin, the client loses access immediately.

An older MCP key belongs to one organization and has explicit scopes. Course and organization permissions still apply to the person who created the key. A key created by a tutor does not give that tutor administrator access.

![Connected apps list on the Automation MCP page showing each app's permissions](./images/understand-mcp-limits-and-permissions/overview.webp)

MCP can read and change supported course, cohort, learner, and organization data within the connection's access. Review [MCP access and limits](https://classroomio.com/docs/developers/mcp/access-and-limits) before connecting a client. Remove a connected app under **Automation → MCP → Connected apps** to cut off its access immediately.

Requests are limited per minute for reads, writes, and publishes. The limits depend on your plan and apply to each connected app or key and to the whole organization; the dashboard does not show them. If you hit one, wait a minute and retry. See [MCP access and limits](https://classroomio.com/docs/developers/mcp/access-and-limits#limits) for the numbers. Analytics reads do not consume AI credits; other AI-related product actions may have their own credit rules.

A client can expose data returned by permitted tools to its operator. Limit who can use the client, inspect proposed writes before publication, and remove the connected app, or [revoke an older key](/integrations-and-automation/create-rotate-and-revoke-mcp-keys), when it is no longer needed.
