New MCP keys can no longer be created. To connect a client, [sign in from the client](/integrations-and-automation/connect-claude-code-codex-cursor-or-opencode) instead. Keys you already have keep working, and organization administrators can rotate or revoke them under **Automation → MCP**. The **MCP keys** section appears only while your organization has at least one key.

![MCP keys list with status and key actions](./images/create-rotate-and-revoke-mcp-keys/overview.webp)

MCP keys are organization scoped. Review [permissions and limits](/integrations-and-automation/understand-mcp-limits-and-permissions) before leaving one in use.

## Rotate or revoke

Open the key's action menu and choose **Rotate** to replace a secret. Copy the new value and update each client using that key; the previous secret stops working. Choose **Revoke** to stop a key entirely. Confirm the action and check that its status changes.

If you lose a secret, ClassroomIO cannot show it again. Rotate the key, or revoke it and connect the client by signing in. Never paste a live key into a support ticket or screenshot.
