Open a course's **Submissions** tab to review student exercise work. Use **Analytics** for course-wide lesson and exercise completion rates; a submitted exercise is one part of progress, not necessarily a completed course.

![Course Submissions page filtered to one exercise, with Submitted, In Progress, and Graded columns](./images/view-submissions-and-completion-data/overview.webp)

## Find a submission

From **Courses**, open the course and select **Submissions** in its sidebar. Choose the exercise or submission you want to inspect. The submission view shows the student's answers and any available grading actions. If **Submissions** reports that access is denied, ask an organization administrator or course owner for grading access.

For a student-by-student overview, open **Analytics** on the same course. The **Students** card lists each student with progress, a score column for every exercise, and last login. Select a student's name to open their course profile.

## Understand the counts

The **Completion rate** card on course **Analytics** is the share of enrolled students who finished the course. A student's exercise column compares their best graded attempt with the exercise; **Pending** means an attempt is waiting for a grade. Use the submission itself to check grading status and feedback; an answer can be submitted before its grade is final.

For scores across the course, open **Marks**. Its export has one column per exercise and an average grade for each student. Export course **Analytics** when you need progress and last-login data instead.
