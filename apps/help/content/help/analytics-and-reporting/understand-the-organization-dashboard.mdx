The organization **Dashboard** is a snapshot of your active courses, enrolled students, certificates, and recent activity. Open **Dashboard** in the organization sidebar after signing in as an administrator or tutor.

![Organization dashboard with certificate, course, student, and top course cards](./images/understand-the-organization-dashboard/overview.webp)

## What the dashboard shows

- **Certificates issued** counts certificate records earned on active courses.
- **Number of courses** counts active courses, including drafts that have not been published.
- **Total students** counts unique students enrolled in at least one active course. Someone in **Audience** with no course enrollment is not included.
- **Top courses** ranks up to five active courses by recorded lesson progress. Its completion figure measures lessons, not exercise submissions.
- **Recent certifications** lists the five newest certificate recipients on active courses.
- **Student login activity** shows the pattern of unique student logins by weekday over the last 90 days.

The snapshot can lag behind a newly completed lesson. Course creation, enrollment, and certificate issuance update their headline figures immediately; lesson progress may take up to ten minutes. The login chart updates less frequently.

For the calculation and exclusions behind each card, see [How the Admin Dashboard Works](/organization-and-team/admin-dashboard). For visitor traffic and enrollment events, open **Marketing** instead.
