Open **Analytics → Academy** in your organization sidebar to see how students are doing across every course and cohort. The page shows organization-wide summary cards, a health check for each cohort, the reasons students need attention, login and growth trends, and your courses ranked by completion.

The live indicator dot next to the page title shows when figures were last updated. If your plan has a student limit, seat usage appears alongside the student growth chart.

Use the **Courses (N)**, **Cohorts (N)** and **Learning paths (N)** switch under the summary cards to switch between course, cohort and learning path figures. The page opens on **Courses**. **Cohorts** appears only when your organization has a cohort, and **Learning paths** only when it has a learning path; with neither, the switch is hidden and only the course figures appear. The summary cards, **Needs attention**, the growth and login charts, and **Courses by completion** stay in every view.

![Organization Academy page on Courses with summary cards and Courses at a glance](./images/monitor-students-across-your-organization/overview.webp)

## Before you start

Only organization administrators can open **Academy**. Tutors and students don't see the item, and opening its URL without access shows "You don't have access to these analytics".

The page counts students enrolled in any of your organization's courses, including courses that aren't published yet. Deleted courses and course templates are not counted, and neither are students whose organization membership is archived.

## When there's nothing to show yet

If your organization has no students, a notice appears at the top asking you to add students to your organization, then enrol them in courses or cohorts. The notice offers an inline link to **Add students** to open your organization's audience page. The summary cards show zeros or dashes, and the cards below show their usual empty states. The range picker remains enabled.

The page keeps checking in the background, so the notice disappears and cards fill in on their own when the first student joins. Reload the page if you need the change immediately.

:::info[Looking for landing page traffic?]
Visitor, enrollment, and funnel figures for your organization's public landing page are on **Analytics → Marketing**. See [Read your marketing overview](/analytics-and-reporting/read-your-marketing-overview).
:::

## Read the summary cards

The five cards under the page header summarize students' work across all of your courses.

| Card                       | What it shows                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Logged in, last 7 days** | Students who signed in at least once in the past 7 days. The hint shows them as a share of enrolled students. Under a range, the title and count use the last N days.                                                            |
| **Average progress**       | The mean share of required lessons and exercises each student has completed, across all of their courses. Items with a **None** completion rule are left out. The hint shows the change this week, or across the selected range. |
| **Completions**            | Course completions: each student who finished a course counts once for that course. Under **All time**, the hint shows the share of enrolled students. Under a range, it shows how many completions happened in that range.      |
| **Certificates issued**    | Certificates students have earned. Under **All time**, the hint shows them as a share of completions. Under a range, it shows how many certificates were issued in that range.                                                   |
| **Needs attention**        | Students who are not finished and are at risk: no login for 2 or more days or no login at all, an average score under 50%, or overdue work. The card turns red when at least 5% of students need attention.                      |

## Choose a time range

Use the range picker beside **Refresh** to choose **All time**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, or **Last 90 days**. **All time** is the default. A range covers today and the days before it, by UTC day.

A range changes what students _did_ in that window. Where students _stand_ is always as of now.

- **Follow the range:** **Logged in** (the title becomes **Logged in, last N days**), **Average progress**, **Completions**, **Certificates issued**, **Enrolled students vs target**, and **Students logged in per day**.
- **Stay current:** **Needs attention**, **Courses at a glance**, **Students by status**, **Average score, by course**, **Courses by completion**, **Cohorts at a glance**, **Students by cohort health**, **Average score, by cohort**, **Learning paths at a glance**, **Path students by status**, and **Completion rate by path**.

Cards that stay current carry a **Now** tag. Its tooltip explains that the range does not change that card. Your range choice carries over when you move between analytics pages, and resets on a full reload.

On the Free plan, only **Last 7 days** is available and the page opens on it. The other ranges show a lock, and selecting one opens the upgrade dialog.

## Compare your courses

**Courses at a glance** is the default table. It lists courses five per page in title order. Each row shows **Course**, **Created**, **Students**, **Avg progress**, **Avg score**, **Completed**, **Took a pass-mark exercise**, and **Pass rate**. Select a course's name to open its **Analytics** page. When there are more than five courses, the pager reads **Showing 1–5 of N courses in organization**.

**Students by status** groups all students into **Completed**, **At risk**, **Lagging**, and **On track**. **Average score, by course** shows the courses on the current page in title order with each course's average score. **Courses by completion** ranks courses by the share of enrolled students who completed them, five per page. When there are more than five, the pager reads **Showing 1–5 of N courses in organization**.

![Courses at a glance table outlined, with student counts, progress, scores, and completion](./images/monitor-students-across-your-organization/courses.webp)

## Compare your cohorts

Select **Cohorts (N)** under the summary cards to see these figures.

**Cohorts at a glance** lists every cohort that isn't archived, including inactive ones, five per page, newest first. Each row shows the cohort's name, when it was **Created**, its **Students** (with invited students underneath when there are any), a progress bar, **Avg score**, **Overdue** students, and a **Health** pill. Empty cohorts are included, so a newly created cohort appears before anyone joins. A cohort with no students yet shows **No students yet** under **Avg progress**, **–** for **Overdue**, and a muted **No students** pill under **Health**; its **Students** count stays at 0, with any invitations underneath. Select a cohort's name to open that cohort's own **Analytics** page.

When there are more than five cohorts, the pager under the table reads **Showing 1–5 of N cohorts in organization** and moves through the rest. Every cohort that isn't archived appears on some page. An archived cohort does not appear here; open it from **Cohorts** to see its own dashboard.

The **Health** column rates each cohort from the same row, checking these rules in order:

- **Completed:** at least 95% of its students completed every course.
- **At risk:** at least 20% of its students are at risk.
- **Lagging:** its average score is under 65%.
- **On track:** every other cohort.

**Students by cohort health** is its own card. It adds up the students in each health group from the cohorts on the current page (five by default) and shows the total in the centre. The note names those cohorts, for example "The 5 cohorts in the table above." Cohorts with no students add nothing to the donut; the note names them afterwards, for example "Not counted: Cohort 2 (no students yet)."

**Average score, by cohort** shows the cohorts on the current page, newest first, with each cohort's average score. A student's score is the best graded attempt on each exercise they have taken. A cohort with no students shows **No students yet** instead of a bar; a cohort with students but no graded attempts shows **No graded attempts**.

![Cohorts at a glance table outlined, with health pills, and the students by cohort health card below](./images/monitor-students-across-your-organization/cohorts.webp)

## Compare your learning paths

Select **Learning paths (N)** under the summary cards to see these figures. They count the students of each learning path that hasn't been deleted, published or not, leave out people you deactivated in your organization, and match the path's own **Analytics** page.

**Learning paths at a glance** lists your learning paths, five per page, in name order. Each row shows the path's name, its **Courses**, its **Students**, a progress bar for **Avg progress**, the **Completion rate** (students who finished the path), **Active (14 days)** (students who completed a lesson or submitted an exercise in one of its courses in the last 14 days), and the path **Certificates** issued. A path with no students yet shows **No students yet** under **Avg progress** and **–** for the rates. Select a path's name to open that path's own **Analytics** page. When there are more than five paths, the pager reads **Showing 1–5 of N learning paths in organization**.

**Path students by status** adds up the students of every learning path who have **Not started**, are **In progress**, or have **Completed** their path, and shows the total in the centre. A student on two paths counts once on each.

**Completion rate by path** ranks up to five learning paths that have students by the share of their students who completed the path.

## See why students need attention

**Needs attention, by reason** counts the students who are not finished for each reason:

- **No login, 2+ days**
- **Average score under 50%**
- **Overdue**
- **Video attendance under 60%**

A student can meet more than one reason, so these counts can add up to more than the **Needs attention** card.

## See the students behind a number

Select the **Needs attention** card, or a slice or bar that counts students, to open a side sheet listing exactly those students. You can do this on each slice of **Students by status** and **Students by cohort health**, and on each bar of **Needs attention, by reason**. The sheet title states the filter in words, for example **20 students · At risk**.

Each row shows progress, average score, last login, and status. Select a student's name to open their details sheet. **Export as CSV** downloads the same students, in the same columns as the student exports, and the menu beside it offers Excel, PDF, and HTML. Long lists are paged ten students at a time.

Point at a widget, or move keyboard focus into it, to show its buttons at its top right. On a touch screen they are always visible.

- **Expand** opens the sheet. It appears on **Students by status**, **Students by cohort health**, and **Needs attention, by reason**. On **Students by status** and **Needs attention, by reason**, the sheet opens on the first group that has students and shows a switcher at the top, so you can move between statuses or reasons without closing it. On **Students by cohort health**, it lists the students in the cohorts shown.
- **Download CSV** downloads the rows behind the widget as displayed, including only the page of a paged widget that you are looking at. Every widget has it. The summary cards at the top have no buttons; the **Needs attention** card opens the sheet when you select it.

**Students by cohort health** counts the students who belong to cohorts with each health. A slice is therefore the students in at-risk cohorts, not the students who are at risk. The sheet says so. If a student belongs to more than one cohort, the chart counts them once per cohort while the list shows them once, and the sheet notes the difference.

## Follow growth and logins

**Enrolled students vs target** shows the running total of students who have joined your organization. Under **All time**, it covers the last six weeks. A flat stretch means no students joined that week. Set a target with **Set target**, or change it with **Edit target**. The target appears as a dashed line, and the vertical axis extends to your plan's seat limit when one is set. When your plan has a student limit, the **Seats** figure above the chart shows the count that limit uses. When some of those seats belong to students who were added or invited but have not joined yet, it adds **N not joined yet**. Those students hold a seat unless their invitation was revoked or has expired, and they appear on the chart once they join.

![Enrolled students vs target card with an arrow at the Seats label showing 143 of 10,000](./images/monitor-students-across-your-organization/seats.webp)

Under **Last 7 days** and **Last 30 days**, the chart shows one point per day. Under **Last 90 days**, it groups the points by week.

**Students logged in per day** counts the students who signed in on each of the last 7 days. Under a range, it covers the last N days. Days run in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC), and a sign-in to any ClassroomIO organization counts, not only yours. The chart draws as a line, and its date labels are thinned as the range widens so they stay readable.

![Students by cohort health, enrolled students vs target, students logged in per day, and average score by cohort](./images/monitor-students-across-your-organization/trends.webp)

## Keep figures current

The live indicator dot shows when the figures were last updated. Select **Refresh** to request the latest data for every card and for the course and cohort lists. While the page stays open and visible, it also refreshes on its own about every minute.

## Troubleshoot missing figures

- **Analytics couldn't load:** select **Try again**. If the page still fails, reload it.
- **The page says there are no students yet:** add students to your audience and enrol them in courses or cohorts, then wait for the page to refresh or select **Refresh**.
- **A chart says there is no data yet:** no student has matching activity yet, for example no graded attempts for **Average score, by cohort**.
- **A cohort says No students yet:** the cohort has no active students. Invite students to the cohort, then wait for the page to refresh.
- **A cohort is missing from Cohorts at a glance:** the table pages through cohorts that aren't archived five at a time. Use the pager to reach older cohorts. An archived cohort does not appear; open it from **Cohorts** to see its own dashboard.
- **A student is missing from the counts:** confirm that the student is enrolled in at least one course and that their organization membership is active.
