Open **Stats → Analytics** in your organization sidebar to see how students are doing across every course and cohort. The page shows organization-wide summary cards, a health check for each cohort, the reasons students need attention, login and growth trends, and your courses ranked by completion.

## Before you start

Only organization administrators can open **Analytics**. Tutors and students don't see the item, and opening its URL without access shows "You don't have access to these analytics."

The page counts students enrolled in any of your organization's courses, including courses that aren't published yet. Deleted courses and course templates are not counted, and neither are students whose organization membership is deactivated.

:::info[Looking for landing page traffic?]
Visitor, enrollment, and funnel figures for your academy landing page moved to **Stats → Marketing**. See [Read your organization analytics overview](/analytics-and-reporting/read-your-organization-analytics-overview).
:::

## Read the summary cards

The line under the page title counts the students in your organization. The cards below it summarize their work across all of your courses.

| Card                       | What it shows                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Logged in, last 7 days** | Students who signed in at least once in the past 7 days. The hint shows them as a share of all students.                            |
| **Average progress**       | The mean share of lessons and exercises each student has completed, across all of their courses.                                    |
| **Completions**            | Course completions: each student who finished a course counts once for that course. The hint counts how many of them are certified. |
| **Needs attention**        | Students who are not finished and are at risk: no login for 2 or more days, an average score under 50%, or overdue work.            |

## Compare your cohorts

**Cohorts at a glance** lists each active cohort with its number of students, average progress, average score, and how many students are overdue. Select a cohort's name to open that cohort's own **Analytics** page.

Students enrolled in a course outside any cohort are grouped under **No cohort**, which has no link. A student who belongs to two cohorts counts once in each.

The **Health** column rates each cohort from the same row, checking these rules in order:

- **Completed:** at least 95% of its students completed every course.
- **At risk:** at least 20% of its students are at risk.
- **Lagging:** its average score is under 65%.
- **On track:** every other cohort.

The chart above the table adds up the students in each health group, so you can see how many students sit in cohorts that need help.

## See why students need attention

**Needs attention, by reason** counts the students who are not finished for each reason:

- **No login, 2+ days**
- **Average score under 50%**
- **Overdue**
- **Video attendance under 60%**

A student can meet more than one reason, so these counts can add up to more than the **Needs attention** card.

## Follow growth and logins

**Learners added** shows the running total of active students in your organization at the end of each week, for the last 12 weeks. A flat stretch means no students joined that week.

**Learners logged in per day** counts the students who signed in on each of the last 14 days. Days run in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC), and a sign-in to any ClassroomIO academy counts, not only yours.

## Compare cohort scores and course completion

**Average score by cohort** ranks up to 10 cohorts by their students' average score. A student's score is the best graded attempt on each exercise they have taken.

**Courses by completion** ranks up to 10 courses by the share of enrolled students who completed them.

## Keep figures current

The header shows when the figures were last updated. Select the refresh icon (**Refresh**) to request the latest data. While the page stays open and visible, it also refreshes on its own about every minute.

## Troubleshoot missing figures

- **Analytics couldn't load:** select **Try again**. If the page still fails, reload it.
- **A chart says there is no data yet:** no student has matching activity yet, for example no graded attempts for **Average score by cohort**.
- **A cohort is missing from Cohorts at a glance:** the cohort is archived, or none of its students are enrolled in its courses.
- **A student is missing from the counts:** confirm that the student is enrolled in at least one course and that their organization membership is active.
