Open a learning path and select **Analytics** in its sidebar. The page uses the same summary cards, charts and student list as [cohort analytics](/analytics-and-reporting/track-a-cohort-with-analytics), worked out for the students on the path and the courses in it. The path's **Course funnel** and **Where students are stuck** cards sit at the bottom.

## Before you start

You need to be an organization administrator or a tutor assigned to the path. Other people can't open the page.

The figures count the path's students who haven't been removed from it, and leave out people you deactivated in your organization. They come from the courses currently in the path. These are the same students counted under **Learning paths** in [organization analytics](/analytics-and-reporting/monitor-students-across-your-organization#compare-your-learning-paths).

If the path has no students yet, a notice at the top links to **Invite students**, which opens the path's people page.

## Read the page

- **Range and course:** The range picker sets the window for cards that measure activity. The **Course** picker narrows the cards and charts to one course in the path, and adds charts for that course such as **Progress distribution** and **Scores and attendance by section**.
- **Summary cards:** **Logged in today**, **Average progress**, **Average score**, **Overdue** and **Completed**. **Completed** counts students who finished every course in the path and shows how many hold a certificate.
- **Courses and students:** **Courses in this path**, **Students by status**, **Course details**, **Needs attention, by reason**, **Overdue students**, **Hardest questions** and the **Students** table. Status and attention reasons follow the [cohort rules](/analytics-and-reporting/track-a-cohort-with-analytics#understand-status-and-attention-reasons).
- **Path cards:** **Course funnel** shows how many students reached and completed each course, in path order. **Where students are stuck** lists the lessons and exercises holding up the most students. These two cards don't follow the range or course pickers.

**Overdue students** lists overdue work but has no reminder buttons. Goal reminders belong to cohorts.
