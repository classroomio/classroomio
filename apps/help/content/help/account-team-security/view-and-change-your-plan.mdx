Open **Settings → Billing** in your primary organization to see the active plan and the billing provider. Additional organizations show a notice that their billing belongs to the primary account. Only organization admins see **Open billing**, and only for plans billed through a billing provider (manually granted plans show no button); other members see a notice that only admins can manage billing.

![Billing page showing the current plan and plan options](./images/view-and-change-your-plan/overview.webp)

## Review a plan change

1. Check the active plan under **Settings → Billing**.
2. Select **Upgrade** to view available plans, or **Open billing** (organization admins only, provider-billed plans) to manage the existing subscription in the billing portal.
3. Review the quoted price, effective date, renewal, and any limits shown before confirming in the checkout or portal.
4. Return to ClassroomIO and verify that the plan displayed in Billing has updated.

If your plan was granted manually, Billing shows a notice instead of **Open billing**, and there is no self-serve way to change it. Contact ClassroomIO support to change or cancel it.

A lower plan may allow fewer organizations or students. Check [student and organization limits](/account-team-security/understand-student-and-organization-limits) before changing plans. If you need invoices, payment details, or cancellation, ask an organization admin to use **Open billing**; its options depend on your billing provider. Do not change a subscription based only on a feature banner—read the portal confirmation first.
