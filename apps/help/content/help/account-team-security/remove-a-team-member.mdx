---
title: 'Remove a team member'
description: 'Remove admin or tutor access after checking course responsibilities.'
last_reviewed: '2026-10-21'
---

Organization administrators can remove an admin or tutor from **Settings → Teams**. First check whether that person teaches a course that needs another tutor.

![Team member list with a Remove action](./images/remove-a-team-member/overview.webp)

1. Open the correct organization and go to **Settings → Teams**.
2. Find the person by name or email and check their role or pending-invitation status.
3. Select **Remove** on their row and complete the confirmation.
4. Check the updated team list and assign another tutor in any affected course **People** page.

Removal ends that person's team membership. It does not erase course content, submissions, or discussion history they previously authored. Course enrollment is managed separately in each course's **People** page.

You can invite the person again later. Check the new invitation's role before sending it.
