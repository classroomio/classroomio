---
title: 'Create another organization'
description: 'Add a separate academy under your primary account when your plan allows it.'
last_reviewed: '2026-10-21'
---

The current Settings navigation does not expose organization creation. If you have an Enterprise account and need another organization, contact [ClassroomIO support](mailto:help@classroomio.com) to check your allowance and arrange setup.

![Workspaces screen with the organization creation dialog, which is not linked from Settings](./images/create-another-organization/overview.webp)

Tell support the name and academy subdomain you want. They can confirm availability, your account's organization allowance, and the next steps. The screen above shows the underlying creation form, but there is no link to it in the current Settings sidebar; **Add Organization** in the switcher is marked **Coming soon**.

After support confirms setup, switch to the new organization to configure its profile, sign-in settings, team, and courses. Its data is separate, but its usage contributes to the primary account's allowance and subscription. See [primary and additional organizations](/account-team-security/understand-primary-and-additional-organizations) before inviting people.
