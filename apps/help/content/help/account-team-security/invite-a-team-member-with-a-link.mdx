title: 'Invite a team member with a link'
description: 'Copy a role-specific team invitation link and share it with trusted colleagues.'
last_reviewed: '2026-10-21'
---

A team invitation link lets a colleague join as an **Admin** or **Tutor** without entering their email address. Organization administrator access and a paid plan are required.

![Team settings showing the invite link controls](./images/invite-a-team-member-with-a-link/overview.webp)

1. Open **Settings → Teams**.
2. Select the role to grant. Check [roles and permissions](/organization-and-team/roles-and-permissions) first.
3. Select **Copy invite link**. ClassroomIO creates or retrieves a link for that role and copies its URL.
4. Share it privately with the intended colleagues. They follow it and sign in or create an account to join.

Treat the link as access to your team: anyone with a valid link can try to use it. Review the team list after sharing. If someone joined in error, [remove that member](/account-team-security/remove-a-team-member). For course-only access, use a [student invitation link](/manage-learners/invite-learners-with-a-reusable-link).
