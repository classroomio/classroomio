Deleting an additional organization is a permanent, destructive action. Only an authorized administrator of the primary account should proceed. The primary organization has no delete control on **Workspaces**.

![Confirmation dialog for deleting an additional organization](./images/delete-an-organization/overview.webp)

## Prepare

Check the organization name and site address carefully. Export any student, course, marks, or report data you must retain, and review active domains, integrations, and billing dependencies. Removing the organization also removes access to its academy data for its team and students. Do not assume deleted content or certificates can be recovered.

## Delete

1. Switch to the primary organization and open **Settings → Workspaces**.
2. Find the additional organization you intend to remove and select **Delete** on its row.
3. Read the confirmation and cancel if the name is not the intended one.
4. Confirm deletion, then check that the organization has disappeared from the list.

If deletion fails, the organization remains on the list. Contact ClassroomIO support with the organization name rather than assuming its data was removed.

If you only need someone to stop working in the academy, [remove the team member](/account-team-security/remove-a-team-member) instead. Contact ClassroomIO support before deleting if you need a data-retention or subscription answer specific to your account.
