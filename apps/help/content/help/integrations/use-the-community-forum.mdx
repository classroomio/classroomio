This guide covers asking a question in Community and moderating what gets posted there. For what Community is and how it differs from a course's Newsfeed, see [Community](/reference/community).

## Asking a question

### 1. Open Community

From the sidebar, click **Community**.

### 2. Start a question

Click **Ask Community**. This opens the ask page.

### 3. Fill in the question

Enter a title and the question body, and pick the course the question is about. Every question belongs to a course.

### 4. Publish

Click **Publish**. The question is immediately visible to people in that course and to admins, and they can answer, comment, and vote on it.

## Moderating questions

Moderation in Community covers deleting, editing your own posts, and hiding posts from learners:

- **Deleting** — an admin can delete any question or any answer/comment, using the trash icon next to it and confirming in the dialog that follows. Authors can delete their own question or their own comments the same way.
- **Editing** — only the original author can edit a question, using the **Edit** button at the top of the question page (it switches the title, body, and course link into an editable form; click **Save** to update or **Cancel** to discard). This doesn't extend to comments and answers, which can only be deleted, not edited. It also doesn't extend to admins editing someone else's question: admins can delete another member's post, not edit it.

- **Hiding** — in the ClassroomIO mobile app, course tutors and organization admins can hide a question or answer from learners. Hidden posts stay visible to tutors and admins and can be shown again. Hiding is not available in the web Community screen.

There's no separate report or flag action in Community.
