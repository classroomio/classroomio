Add signatories in the course certificate editor so an issued certificate identifies who signed it. You can enable one or two signatories, each with a name, role, and optional signature image.

![Certificate editor showing signatory fields and live certificate preview](./images/add-certificate-signatories/overview.webp)

## Add a signatory

1. Open the course's **Certificates → Design** tab and select **Customize design**.
2. Under **Signatories**, turn on **Show signatory 1**.
3. Enter **Signatory 1 name** and **Signatory 1 role**. Upload a **Signature image** if you want a handwritten signature to appear.
4. Turn on and complete **Signatory 2** if a second signer is needed.
5. Check the live preview, then save the design.

Use a signature image you have permission to display. Check that the name and role remain readable on the selected template, especially at smaller sizes.

:::warning[Review before students qualify]
Certificates are generated from the design in effect when students earn them. Finish names and signatures before the first certificate is issued.
:::
