title: Understand locked course content
description: Check whether an item is released and whether sequential progression blocks it when a student cannot open course content.

A student can't open an item for one of two reasons: you haven't released it (they see **Not released yet**), or the course is sequential and an earlier required item is unfinished (they see **Complete previous content first**, and the item's page names the item to finish). Publishing the course does not change either.

## Find the blocking rule

1. Open the course's **Settings → Completion rules** and check **Student progression**. With **Sequential**, the student must complete earlier required items in the saved course order.
2. In **Content**, check whether the lesson or exercise is held back (its lock button's tooltip reads **Release**). Releasing it removes the hold for every student; in a sequential course they still need to finish earlier required items. In a sequential course, a required item that is held back also keeps every later item closed until you release it and the student completes it.
3. If an earlier lesson is unfinished, inspect its **Completion rule**. A **Video watch** rule needs an uploaded video and its configured watch threshold.
4. If an earlier exercise is unfinished, inspect whether it requires **Submitted**, **Passed**, or **None — optional**. Optional exercises never block sequential progression. A passing rule may wait for instructor grading or another attempt.

After changing a rule or releasing an item, save it and test with a separately enrolled demo student. Administrator access can bypass restrictions and give a misleading result.
