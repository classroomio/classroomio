---
title: Review issued certificates
description: Find certificate activity on the organization dashboard and in a course.
last_reviewed: '2026-10-21'
---

Use the organization dashboard to check recent certificate activity. Its **Certificates issued** figure gives an overview, and **Recent certifications** lists the latest awards.

![Organization dashboard showing issued certificates and recent certifications](./images/review-certificate-reports/overview.webp)

To investigate a particular award, open the course's **People** page. Filter students by certificate status (**Earned** or **Not earned**), then open the student's row to review progress and grades. You can inspect the course design and download setting under **Certificates**. A student who qualifies can [download their certificate](/deliver-and-engage/help-a-learner-download-a-certificate) from the learning area.

The dashboard is a recent activity view; it does not provide a dedicated certificate report with filters or an export control. For completion and grade records, use [course analytics](/analytics-and-reporting/review-course-performance-and-learner-progress) and [marks](/deliver-and-engage/view-and-export-marks).
