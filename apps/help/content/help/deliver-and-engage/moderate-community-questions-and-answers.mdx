Use **Community** to read questions from students and respond in the relevant thread. Course questions show their linked course, which helps you keep the answer in context.

![Community question with an answer and moderation menus](./images/moderate-community-questions-and-answers/overview.webp)

1. Open **Community** and choose a question.
2. Read the question and existing answers before replying.
3. Enter your response in **Give an answer** and select **Comment**.
4. Authors can delete their own questions and answers. Organization admins can also delete another member's content. If a delete action is available to you, select it and confirm in the dialog.

Deleting removes that content from the thread. There is no accepted-answer control in the current Community screen, so use a clear reply to explain the resolution. See [Use the Community forum](/integrations/use-the-community-forum) for student participation.
