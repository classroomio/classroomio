Open the invitation link you received from the academy or course team. Confirm that it names the academy or course you expected before continuing.

![Newly joined course open in the student learning view](./images/accept-an-academy-or-course-invitation/overview.webp)

1. For an email-specific invitation, sign in or create an account with the invited email address. For a reusable invitation link, use the account you want to join with.
2. Return to the invitation flow after sign-in. For a course invitation, select **Join course** if the enrollment page asks you to confirm.
3. Open **Home** or **My Learning** to check for your new course. An academy invitation may give you an account before any course is assigned.

A link can expire, reach its use limit, or be revoked. If it no longer works, ask the inviter for a new one. If you signed in with a different email, sign out and retry with the invited address or ask the academy administrator to check the invitation.

## Join from the mobile app

Invitation links open in your browser. To join in the ClassroomIO mobile app instead, copy the invitation link, open the app, and tap **Join** next to **Have an invite link?** on the sign-in screen. Paste the link and tap **Continue**.

If you do not have an account yet, enter your full name, your email address, and a password of at least 8 characters, then tap **Create account**. You can also tap **Continue with Google**. To use an account you already have, tap **Sign in** at the bottom of the screen and sign in there.

The app accepts the invitation as soon as you are signed in and opens **Home**. If you have not started any course yet, Home shows your first course under **Start learning**.

Do not forward an email-specific invitation link to others. For open enrollment, see [Enroll yourself in a course](/learner-guides/enroll-yourself-in-a-course).
