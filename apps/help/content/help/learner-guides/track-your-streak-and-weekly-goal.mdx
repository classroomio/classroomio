Open **Goals** in the ClassroomIO mobile app to see your daily streak, your weekly lesson goal, and your streak milestones.

## Daily streak

Your streak is the number of days in a row you have learned something. A day counts when you complete a lesson, submit an exercise, or are marked present or late at a live class. Days follow your phone's time zone.

Saturday and Sunday never break a streak, so a Monday-to-Friday routine keeps it going. Learning on a weekend still adds to it.

The top of the screen shows your current streak, your best streak, and the last seven days. The flame is lit once you have learned today and faded while today is still open. Days you learned on are filled and joined, and a moon marks weekend days you took off.

## Weekly lesson goal

The weekly goal is the number of lessons you aim to complete each week. Use **−** and **+** to change it. The ring fills with the lessons you have completed since Monday, and the text next to it shows how many are left. You get a notification when you reach the goal.

## Milestones

Milestones mark streaks of 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, and 100 days. A milestone is filled in once your best streak reaches it. The next milestone is shown on its own with a bar for your current streak, and every other milestone shows how many more days you need.

## Streak reminders

On weekday evenings at 7 pm, if you have a streak and haven't learned anything that day, the app sends a reminder. Turn these off under **Profile** › **Notifications**, in **Streaks and goals**.
