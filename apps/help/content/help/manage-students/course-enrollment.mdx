You can let students enroll themselves, invite them by email or a reusable link, or give existing organization students direct access. Choose the enrollment method before sharing the course.

![Course People page with enrollment and invitation controls](./images/course-enrollment/overview.webp)

## Choose an enrollment method

- **Open enrollment for a free course:** [Let students enroll themselves](/manage-learners/let-learners-enroll-themselves). Students sign in or create an account and join from the course landing page.
- **Invite named people:** [Invite students by email](/manage-students/invite-students). New students accept their invitation and create an account if needed.
- **Share one invitation with a group:** [Create a reusable invite link](/manage-learners/invite-learners-with-a-reusable-link). Anyone who receives the active link can join as a student.
- **Enroll an existing organization student:** [Grant direct course access](/manage-learners/add-a-learner-directly-to-a-course). The student does not need to accept another invitation.
- **Enroll many people:** [Import a CSV](/manage-learners/import-learners-from-a-csv-file), or [assign existing students in bulk](/manage-learners/assign-learners-to-courses-in-bulk).
- **Enroll students across several courses:** [Assign them to a cohort](/manage-learners/assign-learners-to-a-cohort).

For a paid course, [verify payment before granting access](/manage-learners/enroll-learners-in-a-paid-course). Invitations and direct assignments grant access without collecting payment.

## Prepare the course

Administrators and tutors with course access manage enrollment from **People**. Organization administrators also manage students from **Audience**.

Review [organization access controls](/manage-students/enrollment-access-control), available student seats, and [course publishing settings](/build-courses/publish-a-course-and-allow-self-enrollment). Joining a course and being able to open its published content are separate checks. An invitation can add a student before the course is published; publish the course before expecting students to study it.

Public courses are different: visitors can read anonymously. Use [Create a public course](/build-courses/create-a-public-course) for that access model.

## Confirm enrollment

Open the course's **People** page and search for the student's name or email. Check their enrollment and progress separately: access does not mean they have started learning. Use [Review a student's course progress](/manage-learners/review-a-learners-course-progress) to follow up.
