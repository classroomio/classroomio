This guide covers the recurring Audience tasks: filtering by usage, bulk actions, assigning students to courses, managing pending invites, and importing or exporting. For what Audience shows and what each status means, see [Audience](/reference/audience).

## Filter students by usage

Use the filter bar above the table to narrow the list. Filters combine, and your selection lives in the page address, so you can bookmark a view or send it to a colleague.

- **Last login** and **Last activity** — not seen in 7, 30, 90 or 180 days, or never.
- **Enrollment** — enrolled, or not enrolled in anything.
- **Completion** — not started, in progress, or completed.
- **Status** — active, deactivated or archived.

The **saved views** beside the filters cover the common questions in one click: never logged in, inactive 90 days, inactive 180 days, enrolled but not started, and archived.

:::note[These filters find dormant students]
"90 days" means **not seen in 90 days**, so these return the people you have not heard from. Recent joiners are held out by default, so nobody is flagged as dormant before they have had a chance to log in.
:::

To act on the result, see [Find and Remove Inactive Learners](/manage-students/find-and-remove-inactive-learners), which walks the whole review-and-archive loop.

## Bulk actions

Tick students in the table, or use **Select all matching** to act on everyone the current filter returns — including when that is thousands of people.

With a selection made, open **Actions**:

| Action         | What it does                                    | Reversible       | Plan seat    |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------ |
| **Archive**    | Removes access, keeps the record                | Yes — Unarchive  | Frees a seat |
| **Deactivate** | Removes access, keeps the record                | Yes — Reactivate | Keeps a seat |
| **Reactivate** | Restores a deactivated student                  | —                | —            |
| **Unarchive**  | Restores an archived student                    | —                | Takes a seat |
| **Delete**     | Permanently removes them from your organization | **No**           | Frees a seat |

Before anything is applied you see a confirmation showing exactly how many students are affected and a sample of who they are, plus an optional **reason** that is recorded against each student so the decision is answerable later.

After a reversible action, an **Undo** button stays available for 15 minutes and applies to exactly the students who changed.

:::caution[Delete is permanent and needs archiving first]
**Delete** is only available for students who are already archived, and asks you to type the number of students to confirm. Their account, submissions and results are kept — they are removed from your organization, not erased.
:::

If the list changed between the confirmation and applying it — someone else archived a student, or a student logged in and left the dormant filter — the action stops and asks you to review again rather than acting on a set you did not see. Runs of more than 1,000 students continue in the background, and you are told when they finish.

## Assign students to courses

<Steps>
<Step>
### Select students

In the Audience table, check one or more students. Only members who already have an account can be selected this way. Rows for invites that haven't been accepted yet don't have a checkbox.

</Step>

<Step>
### Open the assign dialog

Once you have a selection, a bar appears above the table showing how many students are selected and an **Assign to Courses** button. Click it.

</Step>

<Step>
### Pick courses and confirm

In the **Assign to Courses** dialog, pick one or more courses from the list. Leave **Send notification email** checked (it's on by default) to email the students you're assigning, or clear it to give them access without notifying them. Click **Assign**.

</Step>
</Steps>

Assignment happens immediately, and no action is needed from the students. The confirmation message reports how many students were newly assigned, how many already had access to a selected course, and how many emails were sent.

## Manage pending invites

Open the actions menu (the ellipsis icon at the end of a row) for a student whose invite hasn't been accepted yet:

- **Resend invite** — sends a fresh invite email to that address. Available for **Pending**, **Expired**, and **Revoked** invites, so you can also use it to renew an invite that expired or was revoked by mistake.
- **Revoke invite** — cancels the invite so the link stops working. Only available while the invite is still **Pending**.

These actions only show up for invite rows. Once a student has an account, their row no longer has invite actions in this menu.

## Import students

Click **Import Users** in the top right of the Audience page. The button is disabled once your organization reaches its plan's audience limit.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Upload a CSV, or paste addresses

Drop a **CSV** file onto the upload area, or click to choose one. Use **Download the template** if you want the expected shape.

| Requirement |                                                          |
| ----------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Format      | CSV (`.csv`), UTF-8                                      |
| Columns     | `email` (required), `name` and `courses` (both optional) |
| Limit       | 1,000 students and 5 MB per import                       |

The `courses` column takes a comma-separated list of course names, so you can enroll people as you import them. For more than 1,000 students, split the file and import it in parts.

If you would rather not use a file, paste addresses into the field below the upload area instead — one per line, or separated by commas.

</Step>

<Step>
### Review what will happen

Click **Review**. Every row is listed with the outcome it will get. Nothing is created until you continue from this screen.

| Row status                   | Meaning                                             |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Ready**                    | Will be invited                                     |
| **Already in your audience** | Skipped                                             |
| **Invalid email address**    | Skipped                                             |
| **Duplicate in this file**   | Skipped                                             |
| **Staff member**             | Skipped — they are an admin or tutor, not a student |
| **No seats left**            | Skipped — over your plan limit                      |
| **No email address**         | Skipped                                             |

</Step>

<Step>
### Choose course and cohort access

Under **Course Access**, choose whether the imported students get no course assignment, all of your courses, or a specific set. Under **Cohort Access**, make the same choice for cohorts.

</Step>

<Step>
### Submit

Click **Import Users**.

</Step>
</Steps>

New students receive an invitation email. If some rows did not work, you can download just those rows as a CSV from the result screen, fix them, and upload again.

## Export students

Use the **Export as CSV** button above the table, or its dropdown for **Excel**, **PDF** and **HTML**.

The export contains name, email, membership and invite status, courses enrolled, courses completed, progress, last login, last activity and date joined — and it follows whatever you have filtered or selected, so the normal workflow is to filter first, export, circulate for sign-off, then come back and act.

For formats, limits and what the other exports contain, see [Exports](/reference/exports).
