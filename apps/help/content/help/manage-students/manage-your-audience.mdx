Organization administrators use **Audience** to find students, review learning, assign courses, and manage organization access. Choose the task below for its complete instructions.

![Audience with student records and the Filter panel open](./images/manage-your-audience/overview.webp)

## Filter students by usage

[Browse, search, and filter your audience](/manage-learners/browse-search-and-filter-your-audience) to combine activity, enrollment, completion, certificate, membership, and invitation conditions. Saved views help you find students who have never logged in or have been inactive for a set period.

Select a student's name to [review their profile and grades](/manage-learners/view-a-learner-profile-and-activity). The detailed **Activity** timeline is not available yet.

## Bulk actions

[Find and remove inactive students](/manage-students/find-and-remove-inactive-students) explains how to review a list, archive or deactivate memberships, restore access, and use the available undo option.

Archive frees a student seat while keeping the membership record. Deactivate blocks access but keeps the seat. Review the warnings in that guide before permanently deleting memberships.

## Assign students to courses

[Assign students to courses in bulk](/manage-learners/assign-learners-to-courses-in-bulk) to give selected existing students access. This action requires individual row selection; it is unavailable for an all-matching filter selection. Review paid-course access before assigning.

## Manage pending invites

[Manage pending invitations](/manage-learners/manage-pending-invitations) to find pending, expired, or revoked invitations and resend or revoke them. Shared course and cohort invite links have their own controls.

## Import students

[Import students from a CSV file](/manage-learners/import-learners-from-a-csv-file) to preview a list, check invalid rows and seat limits, and assign courses or cohorts. The CSV page sends invitation emails during import; check recipients before confirming.

## Export students

[Export your audience](/manage-learners/export-your-audience) to download selected students or the complete filtered result set as CSV, Excel, PDF, or HTML. Handle the exported personal and learning records with care.
