This guide covers the recurring Audience tasks: filtering by usage, bulk actions, assigning students to courses, managing pending invites, and importing or exporting. For what Audience shows and what each status means, see [Audience](/reference/audience).

## Filter students by usage

Use the filter control above the table to narrow the list. Filters combine, and your selection lives in the page address, so you can bookmark a view or send it to a colleague.

- **No login in** and **No activity in**: 7, 30, 90 or 180 days, or never.
- **Enrollment**: enrolled, or not enrolled in anything.
- **Completion**: not started, in progress, or completed.
- **Status**: active, deactivated or archived.
- **Invite status**: active, pending, expired or revoked.

The **saved views** beside the filters cover the common questions in one click: never logged in, inactive 90+ days, inactive 180+ days, enrolled never started, and archived. Change any filter and the selector reads **Custom filters**.

:::note[These filters find dormant students]
**No login in 90 days** returns the people you have not heard from in that time. Setting either time filter also shows an **Exclude recent joiners** toggle, on by default, which leaves out anyone who joined too recently to count as inactive. See [Excluding recent joiners](/reference/audience#excluding-recent-joiners).
:::

To act on the result, see [Find and Remove Inactive Students](/manage-students/find-and-remove-inactive-students), which walks the whole review-and-archive loop.

## Bulk actions

Tick students in the table, or use **Select all matching** to act on everyone the current filter returns, including when that is thousands of people.

Rows for invites that have not been accepted have no checkbox, because there is no account yet. "Select all matching" is filter-based, so it can still reach them.

![The selection bar above the Audience table, with a red arrow on Select all 19 matching](/help/audience-bulk-actions.webp)

With a selection made, open **Actions**:

| Action         | What it does                                    | Reversible      | Plan seat    |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | --------------- | ------------ |
| **Archive**    | Removes access, keeps the record                | Yes, Unarchive  | Frees a seat |
| **Deactivate** | Removes access, keeps the record                | Yes, Reactivate | Keeps a seat |
| **Reactivate** | Restores a deactivated student                  | n/a             | n/a          |
| **Unarchive**  | Restores an archived student                    | n/a             | Takes a seat |
| **Delete**     | Permanently removes them from your organization | **No**          | Frees a seat |

Before anything is applied you confirm the exact number affected, and you can record an optional **reason** that is stored against each student so the decision is answerable later.

When you use **Select all matching**, the confirmation also lists a few example addresses and warns you if any of them are not archived yet. Ticking rows individually shows the count only, since you can already see who you picked.

**Undo** appears after an archive or a deactivate, for 15 minutes, and applies to exactly the students who changed. Reactivate, unarchive and delete cannot be undone. The Undo button lives on the page, so leaving or reloading it before you use the button clears the offer.

:::caution[Delete is permanent and needs archiving first]
**Delete** only works on students who are already archived, and asks you to type the number of students to confirm. It removes them from your organization and from every course in it, including their course completion records. Their ClassroomIO account and their lesson progress remain.
:::

If the filtered list changed between the confirmation and applying it, a filter-based action stops and asks you to review again rather than acting on a set you did not see. An action on rows you ticked has no such check: anyone who has since been removed is quietly skipped, and anyone already in the target state is reported as unchanged.

Runs of more than 1,000 students continue in the background so you can keep working. Keep the tab open to see the result, because the notification arrives on this page and there is no email.

## Assign students to courses

<Steps>
<Step>
### Select students

In the Audience table, check one or more students. Only members who already have an account can be selected this way. Rows for invites that haven't been accepted yet don't have a checkbox.

</Step>

<Step>
### Open the assign dialog

Once you have a selection, a bar appears above the table showing how many students are selected and an **Assign to Courses** button. Click it.

</Step>

<Step>
### Pick courses and confirm

In the **Assign to Courses** dialog, pick one or more courses from the list. Leave **Send notification email** checked (it's on by default) to email the students you're assigning, or clear it to give them access without notifying them. Click **Assign**.

</Step>
</Steps>

Assignment happens immediately, and no action is needed from the students. The confirmation message reports how many students were newly assigned, how many already had access to a selected course, and how many emails were sent.

## Manage pending invites

Open the actions menu (the ellipsis icon at the end of a row) for a student whose invite hasn't been accepted yet:

- **Resend invite** sends a fresh invite email to that address. Available for **Pending**, **Expired**, and **Revoked** invites, so you can also use it to renew an invite that expired or was revoked by mistake.
- **Revoke invite** cancels the invite so the link stops working. Only available while the invite is still **Pending**.

These actions only show up for invite rows. Once a student has an account, their row no longer has invite actions in this menu.

## Import students

Click **Import Users** in the top right of the Audience page. The button is disabled once your organization reaches its plan's audience limit.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Upload a CSV, or paste addresses

Drop a **CSV** file onto the upload area, or click to choose one. Use **Download the template** if you want the expected shape.

| Requirement |                                     |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Format      | CSV (`.csv`), UTF-8                 |
| Columns     | `email` (required), `name` optional |
| Limit       | 1,000 students and 5 MB per import  |

For more than 1,000 students, split the file and import it in parts.

If you would rather not use a file, paste addresses into the field below the upload area instead, one per line or separated by commas, then click **Review**.

![The import screen with the CSV drop zone, the Download the template link, and the paste field below it](/help/audience-import-upload.webp)

</Step>

<Step>
### Review what will happen

Dropping a file takes you straight here. Every row is listed with the outcome it will get, and nothing is created until you submit from this screen.

![The import preview listing each row with a Ready, Duplicate in file, Invalid email or No email badge, above the course and cohort access options](/help/audience-import-preview.webp)

| Row status               | Meaning                             |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| **Ready**                | Will be invited                     |
| **Already in audience**  | Skipped                             |
| **Invalid email**        | Skipped                             |
| **Duplicate in file**    | Skipped                             |
| **Staff, not a student** | Skipped, they are an admin or tutor |
| **No seats left**        | Skipped, over your plan limit       |
| **No email**             | Skipped                             |

</Step>

<Step>
### Choose course and cohort access

On the same screen, under **Course Access**, choose whether the imported students get no course assignment, all of your courses, or a specific set. Under **Cohort Access**, make the same choice for cohorts.

</Step>

<Step>
### Submit

Click **Import N students**, which counts only the rows marked Ready.

</Step>
</Steps>

New students receive an invitation email.

![The import result screen listing every address with its outcome in a Status column](/help/audience-import-result.webp)

If some rows did not work, **Download the rows that failed** appears here and gives you a CSV of just those, with the reason for each, to fix and upload again.

## Export students

Use the **Export as CSV** button above the table, or its dropdown for **Excel**, **PDF** and **HTML**.

![The Export as CSV button with its dropdown open showing Excel, PDF and HTML](/help/audience-export-menu.webp)

The export contains name, email, membership and invite status, courses enrolled, courses completed, progress, last login, last activity and date joined.

It follows what you have on screen: tick some rows and you get exactly those, otherwise you get everything matching the current filters. So the normal workflow is to filter first, export, circulate for sign-off, then come back and act.

For formats, limits and what the other exports contain, see [Exports](/reference/exports).
