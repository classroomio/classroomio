A cohort groups students and tutors around several courses, shared goals, and a news feed. It is useful when one training group should move through more than one course together.

![Cohort Courses page showing its linked course collection](../reference/images/cohorts/overview.webp)

## What the cohort controls

The cohort connects existing courses; it does not copy them. A course can belong to more than one cohort, and editing that course changes the same content for everyone enrolled in it. Assigning students to the cohort also gives them access to its linked courses. Adding a course later gives existing cohort students access to that course as well.

The cohort has its own **People**, **Settings**, and **Newsfeed**. Its goals can track targets across courses, while lesson completion and grading remain course rules. Students only see a linked course after it is published.

Removing a course from the cohort does not delete it or automatically revoke students' existing course memberships. Check the course roster separately when access should end.
