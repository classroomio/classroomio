An organization owns one ClassroomIO academy and the courses, people, and settings inside it. Your account can belong to more than one organization, but each organization keeps its learning data separate.

![Workspaces list showing a primary organization and an additional academy](../account-team-security/images/understand-primary-and-additional-organizations/overview.webp)

## Organization and academy

The organization is the administrative home for your courses, students, tutors, branding, authentication rules, and reports. Its academy is the site where visitors discover courses and students sign in to learn. An organization has a ClassroomIO subdomain and can use a [verified custom domain](/publish-and-brand/verify-your-domain-and-ssl-certificate).

Switching organizations changes which academy and data you manage. Check the active organization name before you edit settings, invite people, or create a course. A course or student added to one organization does not automatically appear in another.

## Primary and additional organizations

Your first organization is the primary organization for the account. It holds the subscription. Additional organizations use that account plan but have their own academy identity, team, courses, and students. The account's organization and student allowances cover all its organizations together.

You can belong to a separate organization as a team member without making it an additional organization under your account. The organization switcher distinguishes organizations under **Your account** from **Other organizations**.

## Who can manage an organization

An organization administrator manages organization settings, team access, and the academy. Tutors manage the courses they have access to. Students use the academy and learning area; they do not see the administrator's organization settings. See [Roles and permissions](/organization-and-team/roles-and-permissions) for the actions available to each role.
