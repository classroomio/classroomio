If an invitation email is missing, confirm that it was sent to the intended address and that the invitation is still pending. Other course emails may depend on the recipient's notification preferences and the action that triggered them.

![Audience filtered to pending invitations](../manage-learners/images/manage-pending-invitations/overview.webp)

## Check the invitation first

As an organization administrator, open **Audience**, search for the recipient, and filter by **Invite status**. A **Pending** invitation has not completed the join flow; an **Expired** or **Revoked** link needs attention before the recipient can use it. Existing members appear as active students rather than pending invitations.

Check the address, course or cohort assignment, and available student seats before selecting **Open actions → Resend invite**. Ask the recipient to use the newest email and the same address that was invited. If the address is wrong, stop using that invitation and send a new one to the correct address. See [Manage pending invitations](/manage-learners/manage-pending-invitations) for the status and resend actions.

## Check delivery and preferences

Ask the recipient to check spam, quarantine, and any mail rules for the expected message. If a course announcement or other optional notification is missing, have them review **Settings → Notifications** in the student learning area. Those switches control optional email notifications; they do not determine whether an invitation was created.

For a shared course or cohort link, confirm that **Link is active** in the link settings. A reusable link is different from an email invitation and may not have a recipient-specific pending record.

## If it still does not arrive

Record the academy URL, recipient address, message type, approximate send time, and invitation status. Share that context with [ClassroomIO support](/troubleshooting-and-reference/contact-classroomio-support) without forwarding private invitation links or codes.
