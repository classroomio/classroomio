Sign-in problems usually come from using the wrong academy address, email, or sign-in method. If a verification or reset email is missing, check delivery and request a fresh link before trying again.

![Academy sign-in form with the Forgot password link](../learner-guides/images/sign-in-and-reset-your-password/overview.webp)

## Check where and how you sign in

1. Open the academy address from your invitation, or use the academy's verified custom domain. A link for another academy may take you to an account that does not have access to this one.
2. Enter the same email address you used to join. Check for spelling mistakes and aliases.
3. Choose the method your academy allows: email and password, **Google**, or your organization's single sign-on (SSO) provider. A ClassroomIO password reset does not change a password managed by Google or your SSO provider.

If email-and-password sign-in is unavailable, ask the academy administrator which method is enabled. Do not create another account to work around a missing invitation.

## Find a verification or reset email

Check your inbox, spam folder, and any organization quarantine for the newest message. Search for the address you entered, then request a fresh message if the first one did not arrive. Use the newest link; an older, expired, or already-used link may fail.

If the message is still missing, ask your academy administrator to confirm the email address on your invitation and whether your organization blocks external mail. For a password account, follow [Sign in and reset your password](/learner-guides/sign-in-and-reset-your-password).

## Ask for help safely

Tell your academy administrator the academy URL, the email address you intended to use, the approximate time, and the visible error text. You can include a screenshot with personal details hidden. Never send a password, one-time code, reset link, or SSO token.
