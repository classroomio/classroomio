A certificate records that a student met a course's completion rules. A compliance cycle tracks one assigned period of required training, including its due date, status, and validity after completion. A student can earn a certificate during a compliance cycle, but the two records answer different questions.

![Compliance course overview showing student statuses for the current training cycle](../deliver-and-engage/images/understand-compliance-courses/overview.webp)

## Certificate eligibility and download

A student qualifies for a course certificate after completing every lesson and exercise, meeting any exercise passing rule, and finishing by the course completion deadline if one is set. Certificate features require a paid plan. The course's **Certificates → Settings** controls whether an eligible student can download the document; turning download off does not erase the earned certificate record.

Certificates recognize a completed course. See [Set certificate eligibility rules](/deliver-and-engage/set-certificate-eligibility-rules) for the controls that affect issuance, and [View and download your certificates](/learner-guides/view-and-download-your-certificates) for the student view.

## Compliance status and renewal

An assigned student has a current cycle in a compliance course. The cycle can be **Not started**, **In progress**, **Compliant**, or another status that signals an approaching expiry, a grace period, a waiver, or non-compliance. Its due date answers when the work is required; its **Valid until** date answers how long completed training remains current.

When a completed cycle's validity ends, ClassroomIO starts another cycle for the student. The earlier cycle remains in the student's compliance history. Administrators review current obligations under **Compliance → Students** in the course or in the organization compliance report; students see **Current cycle** and **Past cycles** in their course.

A certificate does not, by itself, prove that a student's recurring obligation is current. Check the active cycle's status and dates when reporting compliance.
