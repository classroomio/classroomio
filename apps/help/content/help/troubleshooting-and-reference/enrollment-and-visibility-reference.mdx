Signing in to an academy, finding a course, joining it, and opening its content are separate checks. A student may pass one and still be blocked by the next.

![Course People page with enrollment and invitation controls](../manage-students/images/course-enrollment/overview.webp)

## Follow the access path

1. **Academy account:** Organization signup and authentication settings decide who can create an account and how they sign in.
2. **Course discovery:** Publication and catalog choices decide whether visitors find the course in the academy or **Explore**. A direct link can work even when a course is absent from a listing.
3. **Enrollment:** A student joins by self-enrollment, an email invitation, a reusable link, direct assignment, a cohort, or a completed paid flow. A course invitation can exist before the course is published.
4. **Content access:** Published course material can still contain locked lessons or exercises. Sequential progression and individual availability controls decide when each item opens.

An administrator can confirm course membership under **People**. The student's presence there confirms enrollment, not completion. Use [Choose how students join a course](/manage-students/course-enrollment) for each enrollment method and [organization access controls](/manage-students/enrollment-access-control) for signup restrictions.

Public courses have a different flow: visitors can read without the normal enrolled-student account and course membership path.
