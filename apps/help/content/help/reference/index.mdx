## Why this exists

The glossary is meant to answer one question quickly: what does this term mean in ClassroomIO?

It brings together the core concepts that appear across the product docs, onboarding flows, and feature guides so you can understand the product vocabulary in one place.

## What it covers

It includes terms from:

- the product overview
- feature descriptions
- course and enrollment setup
- organization and admin workflows
- student experience and learning flows
- self-hosting and technical concepts

This makes the glossary a practical companion to the Help Center and a better replacement for a broad, product-style Platform tab.
