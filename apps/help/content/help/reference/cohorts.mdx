A cohort gives a group of people access to several courses and a shared place for learning goals and updates. Use cohorts for an onboarding class, certification group, or training intake.

![Cohort Courses page showing its linked course collection](./images/cohorts/overview.webp)

## What a cohort includes

- **Courses:** existing courses linked to the group. Students gain access to those courses through cohort assignment.
- **People:** students and teaching staff in the group.
- **Settings:** the cohort's name, description, status, and learning goals.
- **Newsfeed:** updates, comments, and reactions for cohort members.

A course can be part of more than one cohort. Linking it does not create a separate copy, so changes to the course affect the same underlying course.

## How students receive access

[Assigning students to a cohort](/manage-learners/assign-learners-to-a-cohort) also enrolls them in its linked courses. [Adding a course later](/manage-learners/add-courses-to-a-cohort) gives existing cohort students access to that course.

Students only see published courses. Your teaching team can link an unpublished course while preparing it, then publish it when it is ready.

Removing a course from a cohort unlinks it from the collection; it does not delete that course or automatically revoke existing course memberships. Review individual access separately when someone should stop studying a course.

## Choose a cohort or a course

Use a course for its lessons, exercises, course progression, and course-specific roster. Use a cohort when a group should share multiple courses, cross-course targets, or announcements.

A cohort is not a separate course copy or a substitute for a course's own completion rules. [Learning goals](/manage-learners/configure-cohort-goals-and-settings) track targets across selected courses.

## Start a cohort

Organization administrators [create a cohort](/manage-students/create-a-cohort), link courses, and invite members. Administrators and the cohort's assigned tutors can manage its teaching workflows.

Once students are working, open the cohort's **Analytics** to see progress, status, attention reasons, and overdue work. See [Track a cohort with analytics](/analytics-and-reporting/track-a-cohort-with-analytics).
