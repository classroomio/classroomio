The search box in the dashboard header and the **Search settings...** box in the settings sidebar open the same search window, titled **Command palette**. Use it to jump to a page, a setting, or a piece of content without browsing menus.

## Open search

You can open it in any of these ways:

- Press `Ctrl` + `K` (`⌘` + `K` on Mac). Press it again to close the window.
- Select the **Search** box in the header. It shows the `⌘ K` hint and is hidden on narrow screens.
- Select **Search settings...** at the top of the settings sidebar.

Typing in either box does not filter in place; the box only opens the search window, where you type your query.

## What it searches

With nothing typed, the window shows **Recent** items (if you have any) and **Suggested** pages. As you type, results appear in groups:

- **Courses**: active courses in your organization, matched against title and description. Templates are not included.
- **Cohorts**
- **Widgets**
- **Tags**
- **Students**: shown only to organization administrators.
- **Settings**: settings pages your role can open, matched against the page name and its section name.

Each group shows up to five matches, and the search starts about a fifth of a second after you stop typing. While it runs you see **Searching...**; if nothing matches you see **No results found**.

The placeholder reads **Search courses, cohorts, widgets, students, tags, settings...**.

## Open a result

Select a result, or move with the arrow keys and press `Enter`. The window closes and opens that page. Courses open the course, cohorts open the cohort's courses, widgets open the widget, tags open the tags page, and a student opens the audience page filtered by that student's email or name.

## Recent results

The last eight results you opened are kept as **Recent**, separately for each organization, in your browser only. Select **Clear recents** to remove them. They do not follow you to another browser or device.

## Search in the learning workspace

When you are in the learning workspace, the same shortcut opens a smaller search with the placeholder **Search your courses, cohorts, and learning pages...**. It searches courses, cohorts, and learning pages only.
