ClassroomIO empowers every organization to tailor their experience to their unique needs. In this guide, you will learn how to customize your organization branding and profile. From your admin dashboard, open **Settings** and choose **Branding**.

![ClassroomIO Organization settings page](/help/customize-organization.webp)

## Update Organization Profile

Easily update your organization's profile by modifying the designated fields in the Organization Profile section of the Settings page. Here, you can update your organization name and logo with your preferred choices.

To update your organization profile details, follow these steps:

### 1. Go to Organization Settings

From your dashboard, click **Settings**, then select **Branding** in the settings sidebar.

### 2. Change Your Organization Name

Fill the Organization name field with your preferred name.

### 3. Click on Upload Image

Click the Upload Image button to select an image for upload.

### 4. Select an Image

Choose a logo or image of your choice from your device.

### 5. Click Update Organization Button

Save the changes by clicking the **Update Organization** button.

By clicking the **Update Organization** button, the updated preferences in the Organization section will be applied across the entire dashboard.

## Customize Domain

Upon onboarding, each organization in ClassroomIO is assigned a custom Domain URL. This URL directs users to the organization's customizable website, making it easy for audiences to access published courses, FAQs, and other relevant information tailored to your organization's preferences. To customize your organization's domain URL, follow these steps:

### 1. Click on "Edit Domain"

Click the Edit Domain button to navigate to the domain setup page.

![Custom Domain setup](/help/domain-setup-one.webp)

### 2. Update the URL Field with Your Preferred Choice

Fill the field with your desired URL.

![Edit Custom domain URL](/help/domain-setup-two.webp)

### 3. Save Changes

Click on Update Domain to save the changes.

> You can click on **View Site** to be redirected to your Organization website with the updated URL.

![Edit Custom domain URL](/help/domain-setup-three.webp)

For full custom domain setup (DNS, verification, which links use your domain), see [Set Up a Custom Domain](/publish-and-brand/custom-domain).

For the public homepage (hero, nav, footer), see [Customize Your Academy Landing Page](/publish-and-brand/org-landing-page).

For favicon and link-preview behavior when sharing your academy URL, see [Share Your Academy (Open Graph & Favicon)](/publish-and-brand/academy-sharing-and-branding).

## Set Brand Color

We understand that every organization has a preferred color that suits their brand, hence we offer organizations the flexibility to choose a theme for their brand from a variety of colors like **blue**, **green**, **orange**, and more. To select a brand color for your organization simply click on any of the provided color theme and this would be automatically applied across your entire application.
