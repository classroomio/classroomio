Open **Settings → Branding** to update your organization's name, logo, and theme.

![Branding settings with the organization name, logo, and theme colors](./images/customize-organization/overview.webp)

## Change the name or logo

1. Enter the new name in **Organization Name**.
2. To change the logo, select **Upload Image** and choose a file from your device.
3. Select **Save changes** in the bar that appears when you make a change. Use **Discard** if you want to keep the saved name and logo.

Check your public academy after saving to see how the new name and logo appear to visitors. The logo also acts as the browser icon when you have not set a [custom favicon](/publish-and-brand/add-a-custom-favicon-or-custom-code).

## Choose a brand color

Under **Theme**, select one of the preset colors or use the **+** picker for a custom color. Theme changes save automatically after you select a color. Open your academy site to check the result.

## Change the academy address

Domain settings are under **Settings → Domains**, separately from Branding. To connect your own hostname, follow [Set up a custom domain](/publish-and-brand/custom-domain). The [DNS record](/publish-and-brand/add-the-required-dns-records) and [verification](/publish-and-brand/verify-your-domain-and-ssl-certificate) guides cover the next steps.
