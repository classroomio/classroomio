Open a course’s **People** page and select a student to review their learning. Use progress, current stage, lesson completion, and exercise results together when deciding whether they need help.

![Course People roster showing student progress and learning stages](./images/review-a-learners-course-progress/overview.webp)

## Find the student

Administrators and tutors with course access open **People** and search for the student's name or email. Use views or **Filter** to focus on students, pending invitations, or people who have never logged in.

Read the **Progress** percentage and current learning stage in the roster. A certificate badge records an earned certificate; it is different from being enrolled. A pending person has not completed the account or invitation flow and does not have the same learning record.

## Open their course record

Select the student's row, or use its action menu and **View**. Review the available lesson and exercise details to see completed work and scores. Open an exercise submission when you need to inspect or grade the student's answers.

Progress and grades answer different questions. A completed lesson does not guarantee a passing exercise score, and an exercise submission can still be awaiting grading. Check the course's [progression and completion rules](/create-and-deliver/course-progression) before treating one percentage as proof that every requirement was met.

## Review learning across courses

Organization administrators can open [the student's Audience profile](/manage-learners/view-a-learner-profile-and-activity) for all enrolled courses, course cards, and a **Grades** view. The profile's detailed **Activity** timeline is not yet available; use Audience login and activity dates for inactivity checks.

## Follow up on missing progress

First confirm that the student is enrolled, the course is published, and required lessons or exercises are unlocked. Check whether they signed in with the invited email. If they have not joined yet, [manage the pending invitation](/manage-learners/manage-pending-invitations).

Use [Audience inactivity filters](/manage-students/find-and-remove-inactive-students) when you need to review many students rather than one course record.
