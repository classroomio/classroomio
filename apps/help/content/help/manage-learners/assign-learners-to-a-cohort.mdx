Use a cohort’s **People → Invite** dialog to add existing students, invite new students by email, or share an invite link. Cohort assignment also grants access to its linked courses.

![Cohort Invite Members dialog on the Add Students tab, with existing organization students above the Invite new students form](./images/assign-learners-to-a-cohort/overview.webp)

## Before assigning members

Organization administrators and the cohort's administrators or tutors can manage student invitations. Only organization administrators can browse the existing organization student picker and team tutor picker.

Review the cohort's **Courses** first. Assigning a student grants access to those courses, including paid courses without collecting payment. Students only see courses once they are published.

## Add existing organization students

As an organization administrator, open the cohort and select **People → Invite → Add Students**. Search **Add existing org students**, select the people to add, review the email notification checkbox, and select **Grant Access**.

Existing students receive membership directly. They do not need to accept a new invitation. Check the cohort **People** list and linked course rosters to confirm access.

## Invite new students

In **Add Students**, find **Invite new students** and enter email addresses in **Email Addresses**. Choose whether to send invitation emails, then select **Invite Students**.

New people get one academy invitation that carries this cohort, and each new email takes a student seat while the invitation is pending. They join the cohort and its linked courses when they accept, creating an account if needed. Check [student seats and signup policies](/manage-students/enrollment-access-control) if they cannot join. Organization administrators can use [CSV import](/manage-learners/import-learners-from-a-csv-file) with **Cohort Access** for a larger list.

People who have not accepted yet appear in cohort **People** after the joined members. Each invited row is marked **Pending**, or **Expired** once the invitation has lapsed. To show only them, select the filter and choose **Status → Invited**. Invited people are visible to the cohort's team and organization administrators, not to students. Open the row's **Open actions** menu and select **Resend invite** to send a fresh invitation email, or **Remove from invitation** to take this cohort off the invitation. Other courses and cohorts on it stay; if nothing else is left, the invitation is revoked and its seat freed. An **Expired** row offers only **Resend invite**, which needs a free student seat. Older email-only rows show **Resend invite** and **Remove from cohort**.

![Cohort People list with joined members first, then Expired and Pending invited rows, and the actions menu open on a pending row](./images/assign-learners-to-a-cohort/invited-members.webp)

## Share one link

Select **Invite link → Create invite link**, then **Copy invite link**. Anyone receiving the active link can join this cohort and its linked courses. [Disable the link](/manage-learners/invite-learners-with-a-reusable-link) when you no longer want it used.

## Add tutors or remove members

Organization administrators can use **Invite Tutors** to select tutors from the organization and finish the assignment. For removal, find the person in cohort **People**, open **Open actions → Remove from cohort**, and review the confirmation carefully.

Removing cohort membership does not automatically remove their existing course memberships. Review [course access](/manage-learners/add-and-remove-people-from-a-course) separately when a student should leave both the group and its courses.
