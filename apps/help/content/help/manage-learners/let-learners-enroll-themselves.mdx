Turn on **Students can enroll themselves** in course **Settings → Access**, publish the course, and share its course landing page. Students then sign in or create an account to enroll.

![Course Access settings showing self-enrollment and publishing controls](./images/let-learners-enroll-themselves/overview.webp)

## Before you start

You need administrator or tutor access to the course. Normal self-enrollment is for free courses; [paid courses require payment verification and an invitation or assignment](/manage-learners/enroll-learners-in-a-paid-course).

Check your organization's [signup and enrollment policies](/manage-students/enrollment-access-control). **Internal Enrollment Only** prevents people outside the organization from enrolling themselves. Student seat limits still apply.

## Open self-enrollment

### 1. Enable enrollment

Open the course, select **Settings → Access**, and turn on **Students can enroll themselves**.

### 2. Publish the course

Turn on **Publish Course**, then select **Save changes**. For a compliance course, set its required completion deadline before publishing.

Publishing does not automatically unlock every lesson or exercise. Review the content students should be able to open.

### 3. Share and test the course page

Open **View Course Site**. If a course link is not available yet, [set up the course landing page](/publish-and-brand/course-landingpage).

Test with a separate demo student account. The student follows the enrollment action on the course page, signs in or creates an account, and joins. Confirm their name appears under course **People** and that they can open the published content in their LMS.

## Close self-enrollment

Turn off **Students can enroll themselves** and save. Existing enrollment stays in place. Invitations and direct assignments remain separate ways to grant access, so [disable shared invite links](/manage-learners/invite-learners-with-a-reusable-link) as well if you want to stop those joins.
