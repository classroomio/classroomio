title: Ask a Question in Community
description: How to post a question, tie it to a course, and what happens when an admin moderates it.
---

If your academy has Community enabled, you can ask questions and get answers from admins and other students.

## Posting a question

1. From the sidebar, click **Community**.
2. Click **Ask Community**.
3. Enter a title and your question, and pick the course it is about.
4. Click **Publish**.

Your question is visible right away to people in the linked course and to admins, and they can answer, comment, or vote on it.

## Editing or deleting your own post

You can edit your own question at any time using the **Edit** button at the top of the question page — this updates the title, body, and course link. You (or an admin) can also delete your question or your own comments using the trash icon.

:::info[Comments and answers can't be edited]
Only questions can be edited, and only by their original author. Answers and comments can be deleted, but not edited — if you need to correct one, delete it and post a new one.
:::

## If an admin removes your post

Admins can delete any question or answer in Community, including yours, but they can't edit your posts for you. There's no separate flagging step. Moderation in Community is delete (by you or an admin), edit (by you only), and, in the ClassroomIO mobile app, hiding a post from learners (by tutors or admins). A hidden post disappears from your Community list.
