Once you sign in, you land in your LMS dashboard. Here's what each area is for.

| Area            | Path              | What it shows                                  |
| --------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Home**        | `/lms`            | Courses you're enrolled in, with your progress |
| **My Learning** | `/lms/mylearning` | Enrolled courses only, filterable by status    |
| **Explore**     | `/lms/explore`    | Published courses you haven't joined yet       |
| **Cohorts**     | `/lms/cohorts`    | Cohorts (multi-course tracks) you belong to    |
| **Community**   | `/lms/community`  | Ask and answer questions (if enabled)          |
| **Settings**    | `/lms/settings`   | Your profile and connected integrations        |

## Home vs. My Learning

**Home** is where you land after signing in — a quick view of every course you're taking, with progress at a glance. **My Learning** is the same set of courses with better filtering, so if you're juggling several courses use its **In progress** / **Complete** tabs to narrow things down.

## Explore

**Explore** lists courses in your academy that you haven't joined yet. It's a catalog, not an enrollment gate — a course can show up here even if you can't self-enroll in it. If **Enroll** isn't clickable, see [Join a Course](/student-guides/join-a-course) for the other ways to get access.

## Cohorts

A cohort groups several courses under one shared track (for example, an onboarding program). If you're part of one, it shows up under **Cohorts** — separate from your individually enrolled courses on Home and My Learning.
