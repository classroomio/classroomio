Everything you need to build and run your academy: creating courses and exercises, running live
classes, enrolling students, publishing your landing pages, and managing your organization and
team.

This resource is for people running a ClassroomIO academy day to day. For self-hosting,
integrating with the API, or contributing code, see the [developer docs](https://classroomio.com/docs).
