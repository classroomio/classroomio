ClassroomIO offers a no-cost plan, Early Adopter, and Enterprise. Choose based on the number of students you need, the features required to publish and administer your academy, and whether you need multiple organizations or enterprise identity controls.

:::note[Free and Basic name the same plan]
The dashboard calls the no-cost plan **Free**. The public pricing page currently calls it **Basic**.
:::

## Plans at a glance

| Plan              | Current price                   | Best for                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Free / Basic**  | $0                              | Trying ClassroomIO or running a small academy without paid publishing features                                                            |
| **Early Adopter** | $35 monthly or $350 annually    | Growing academies that need more students, video uploads, certificates, custom branding, and a custom domain                              |
| **Enterprise**    | Contact ClassroomIO for pricing | Accounts that need unlimited students and API requests, multiple organizations, Single Sign-On (SSO), or deployment and licensing options |

Prices and packaging can change. Check the [ClassroomIO pricing page](https://classroomio.com/pricing) before purchasing.

## Capacity and usage comparison

| Limit                                                               | Free / Basic | Early Adopter | Enterprise |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | -----------: | ------------: | ---------: |
| Students                                                            |           20 |        10,000 |  Unlimited |
| Included AI credits each month                                      |      500,000 |     3,000,000 | 15,000,000 |
| Organizations under one account, including the primary organization |            1 |             1 |          4 |

Student and organization allowances are hard limits. ClassroomIO blocks an action that would exceed the applicable limit. Included AI credits are metered usage: they reset at the start of each calendar month, and purchased credits can extend the available balance.

See [Understand usage and plan limits](/get-started/understand-usage-and-plan-limits) for counting rules and what happens at each limit.

## Feature comparison

| Feature                                         | Free / Basic         | Early Adopter           | Enterprise                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Courses                                         | Unlimited            | Unlimited               | Unlimited                      |
| ClassroomIO Model Context Protocol (MCP) access | Included             | Included                | Included                       |
| Additional administrators and tutors            | Not included         | Unlimited collaborators | Included                       |
| Direct video uploads                            | Not included         | Included                | Included                       |
| Course certificates                             | Not included         | Included                | Included                       |
| Custom branding                                 | ClassroomIO branding | Included                | Included                       |
| Custom domain                                   | Not included         | Included                | Included                       |
| Public API                                      | Not included         | Included with limits    | Unlimited requests             |
| Multiple organizations                          | Not included         | Not included            | Up to four total organizations |
| Single Sign-On (SSO)                            | Not included         | Not included            | Included                       |

Some features also have their own configuration or operational limits. A feature marked as included does not remove file-size rules, API rate limits, course access rules, or other safeguards shown in the product.

## Choose Free / Basic when

Use the no-cost plan when you want to create unlimited courses for a small student audience and do not need paid publishing or administration features. It includes 20 students, 500,000 monthly AI credits, MCP access, and the course builder without direct video uploads.

The Free plan is also the fallback when a paid subscription ends. ClassroomIO does not delete your organization because its plan changes, but Free limits and feature gates apply.

## Choose Early Adopter when

Use Early Adopter when one organization is enough and you need to serve more students or publish with your own brand. It raises student capacity to 10,000 and monthly AI credits to 3 million, and it adds direct video uploads, certificates, custom branding, a custom domain, and limited public API access.

Early Adopter subscriptions are available monthly or annually from the in-app upgrade flow.

## Choose Enterprise when

Use Enterprise when you need any of these requirements:

- unlimited students;
- up to four organizations under one account subscription;
- 15 million included AI credits each month;
- unlimited public API requests;
- Single Sign-On (SSO);
- on-premises deployment or a commercial license;
- enterprise support arrangements.

Enterprise pricing is handled through ClassroomIO's contact flow rather than the self-service Early Adopter checkout.

## What happens when you reach a limit

- **Student limit:** existing students remain in the organization, but ClassroomIO blocks additions that would exceed the plan capacity. Archiving a student frees a seat; deactivating one does not.
- **AI-credit limit:** ClassroomIO uses purchased credits after the included monthly allowance is exhausted. AI actions stop when no included or purchased credits remain.
- **Organization limit:** the primary organization counts toward the allowance. New organization creation stops when the account reaches its total.
- **Feature limit:** the relevant control is unavailable or shows an upgrade requirement. Changing plans does not automatically configure the newly available feature.
