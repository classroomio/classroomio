Once you’ve created your account, work through this checklist to set up your organization, create your first course, and get ready to share your learning experience with customers.

1. **Set your profile picture.** Students and teammates see this next to your name on courses, the forum, and anywhere else you show up in the app.
2. **Set your organization details.** Your organization name and logo appear across your academy, on invite emails, and on the landing page. See [Customize Your Organization](/organization-and-team/customize-organization).
3. **Customize your organization landing page.** This is the public homepage prospects see before they sign in, so it's worth setting up before you invite anyone. See [Customize Your Academy Landing Page](/publish-and-brand/org-landing-page).
4. **Create your first course.** Courses are where lessons, exercises, and students actually live. See [Create Your First Course](/get-started/create-first-course).
5. **Share your course with customers.** Send email invites or allow customers to self-enroll from your landing page, depending on how you want to provide access to the course.
