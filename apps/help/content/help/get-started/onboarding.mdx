Complete these setup tasks after creating your organization so students see a recognizable academy and can open useful course content. You can return to each area from the admin dashboard if you do not finish everything at once.

## 1. Complete your personal profile

Open the account menu, select **Settings**, then open **Profile**. Add your name, username, and profile picture so teammates and students can identify you.

## 2. Add your organization identity

Open **Settings → Branding** and confirm the organization name, logo, and theme. These settings establish the shared identity students see across your academy and learning management system (LMS).

[Customize your academy profile](/get-started/customize-your-academy-profile) explains what each branding area controls.

## 3. Prepare the academy landing page

Open **Distribute → Landing Page** to choose a theme and update the public homepage. Review the hero, navigation, links, and footer before sharing the academy address.

Use [Customize your academy landing page](/publish-and-brand/org-landing-page) for the full editor workflow.

## 4. Create and publish a course

Create a course from the organization home page, then add lessons and exercises. A course can remain unpublished while your team prepares it; publish it when students should be able to discover or access it.

See [Create your first course](/get-started/create-first-course) for the creation and launch sequence.

## 5. Decide how students will join

Choose whether your team will enroll known students, send invitations, share an invite link, or allow self-enrollment. The right method depends on whether access should be controlled by an administrator or initiated by the student.

Review [How course enrollment works](/manage-students/course-enrollment) before inviting students.

## 6. Test the student experience

Use **Open Academy** to inspect the public homepage and **View as student** from a course to review its learner layout. For a final access test, enroll a separate test-student account and follow the same invitation or enrollment path as a real student.

See [Preview your academy as a learner](/get-started/preview-your-academy-as-a-learner) for the differences between these checks.

## Your academy is ready when

- the organization name, logo, and academy address are correct;
- the public landing page has working navigation and calls to action;
- at least one published course contains student-ready content;
- the intended enrollment method works for a test student; and
- the student can sign in and open the expected course.
