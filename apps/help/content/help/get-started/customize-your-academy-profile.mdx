Customize your academy profile from **Settings → Branding**. The organization name, logo, and theme create the shared identity students see across your public academy and learning management system (LMS).

:::info[Profile and landing page are separate]
**Branding** controls the organization identity used across ClassroomIO. **Distribute → Landing Page** controls the content and layout of the public academy homepage.
:::

## Update the organization name and logo

### 1. Open Branding

From the admin dashboard, open the account menu, select **Settings**, then select **Branding**.

### 2. Review the organization profile

In **Organization**, enter the name you want administrators and students to see. Select **Upload Image** to choose the organization logo from your device.

Use a clear logo that remains recognizable at small sizes. The logo can also act as the fallback browser icon when no custom favicon is configured.

### 3. Save the profile

Select **Update Organization**. Open another admin page or your academy to confirm the saved name and logo appear as expected.

## Set the brand theme

In the **Theme** area, choose the brand color used by supported academy and LMS surfaces. Save the organization after changing the theme, then inspect a student-facing page to check contrast and readability.

Some advanced branding options depend on your plan. If a control is locked, [compare plans and feature limits](/get-started/compare-plans-and-feature-limits) before upgrading.

## Continue customizing the academy

The academy profile does not control every public page setting. Use these areas for the rest of the experience:

| To change                                              | Open                          | Guide                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Public homepage hero, navigation, sections, and footer | **Distribute → Landing Page** | [Customize your academy landing page](/publish-and-brand/org-landing-page)                                        |
| Academy subdomain or custom hostname                   | **Settings → Domains**        | [Set up a custom domain](/publish-and-brand/custom-domain)                                                        |
| Browser icon and shared-link preview                   | Branding and sharing settings | [Share your academy](/publish-and-brand/academy-sharing-and-branding)                                             |
| Tabs, banners, and tools students see after signing in | **Settings → Customize LMS**  | [Choose which LMS navigation tabs students see](/publish-and-brand/choose-which-lms-navigation-tabs-learners-see) |

## Verify the result

Select **Open Academy** from the admin header and check the homepage at desktop and mobile widths. Sign in with a test-student account to confirm the same identity carries into the LMS.
