The admin dashboard shows recent ClassroomIO product updates in a card at the bottom of the sidebar, so you can learn about new features without leaving your work.

## Find the updates card

Look at the bottom of the sidebar, above the upgrade prompt and the footer menu. The card shows the newest update you have not opened yet, with a **New** badge, a cover image, a title, and a one-line summary. If the update includes a video, a play icon appears on the card.

The card is hidden when the sidebar is collapsed to icons. It is also hidden when there is no unopened update, or when the changelog cannot be loaded.

## Read an update

### 1. Open the card

Select the card. A window opens with the update's video or cover image, its publish date, any tags, its title, and a summary.

### 2. Choose what to do next

Select **Read the full changelog** to open the full entry in a new tab, or select **Close** to return to the dashboard.

### 3. Move to the next update

Opening an update marks it as seen, and the card then shows the next unopened update. When a stack of cards appears behind the front card, more unopened updates are waiting.

## Where the content comes from

The dashboard fetches the latest changelog entries from the public ClassroomIO changelog, up to five at a time, and lists them newest first. Updates are published by ClassroomIO, not configured by your organization.

Which updates you have seen is remembered in your browser. If you use another browser or device, or clear your browser data, updates you have already opened can appear again.
