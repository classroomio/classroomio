Open **Settings → Billing** from the admin dashboard to upgrade a Free organization or manage an active paid subscription. Billing belongs to the account's primary organization, so an additional organization directs you back to the primary one.

## Before you start

- Use an administrator account for the organization whose subscription you want to manage.
- If the account has multiple organizations, switch to the primary organization first.
- Allow pop-ups for the ClassroomIO admin app because the billing portal opens in a new tab.

## Open Billing

### 1. Open Settings

From the admin dashboard, open the account menu and select **Settings**.

### 2. Select Billing

In Settings, select **Billing**. The **Billing details** page shows one of three states:

- A Free organization shows that it has no active billing and provides an **Upgrade** button.
- A paid primary organization provides **Open billing**.
- An additional organization explains that billing is managed by the primary organization.

## Upgrade from Free

### 1. Select Upgrade

On **Settings → Billing**, select **Upgrade**. You can also use the **Upgrade** control in the organization sidebar.

### 2. Choose a plan and billing interval

The upgrade dialog compares the available plans. Choose **Monthly** or **Annually** for Early Adopter, then select its upgrade action to continue to checkout.

Enterprise uses a contact flow because its price and deployment terms are arranged for the organization.

Review [Compare plans and feature limits](/get-started/compare-plans-and-feature-limits) before purchasing.

### 3. Complete checkout

Complete checkout with the billing provider. ClassroomIO returns you to the page where you started and confirms the plan after the provider activates the subscription.

The upgraded plan name then appears with the organization in the admin dashboard. Newly included features may still need configuration before students can use them.

## Manage an active subscription

### 1. Select Open billing

From **Settings → Billing**, select **Open billing**. ClassroomIO opens the billing provider's customer portal in a new tab.

### 2. Use the available portal actions

The portal is the authoritative place to review the subscription and the actions available for that billing account. Depending on its current state, use it to:

- review the active subscription and renewal information;
- update payment details;
- find invoices or receipts;
- cancel a renewal;
- resume a cancellation before it takes effect, when the portal offers that option.

Return to ClassroomIO after saving a change. Provider updates can take a short time to appear in the admin dashboard.

## What happens after cancellation or a failed renewal

The billing portal shows the effective date for a cancellation. Scheduling a cancellation does not delete the organization, courses, people, or content.

ClassroomIO keeps the paid plan active while the billing provider reports the subscription as active. When the subscription ends and the provider revokes it, the organization falls back to Free plan limits and feature access.

:::warning[Review usage before a paid plan ends]
If the account has more students or organizations than the Free allowance, the lower limits apply after the paid subscription ends. ClassroomIO does not delete students automatically, but it blocks new student additions above capacity. Additional organizations can become read-only when the account exceeds its organization allowance.
:::

Purchased AI-credit balances remain separate from the monthly credits included with a plan. See [Understand usage and plan limits](/get-started/understand-usage-and-plan-limits) for reset and rollover behavior.

## Troubleshooting

### Open billing is not available

Confirm that you are in the primary organization and that its paid plan is active. A Free organization shows **Upgrade** instead, and an additional organization cannot manage the shared subscription.

### The portal does not open

Allow pop-ups for the ClassroomIO admin app, then select **Open billing** again. If it still fails, include the organization name, academy URL, approximate time, and visible error text when contacting support. Do not send card details, passwords, or authentication codes.

### The plan has not updated after checkout

Reload the admin dashboard after checkout finishes. If the old plan still appears after the provider has confirmed payment, contact support with the organization name and the non-sensitive receipt or order reference.
