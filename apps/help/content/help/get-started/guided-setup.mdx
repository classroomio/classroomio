Guided setup takes a new workspace from sign-up to a branded academy with sample courses in a few minutes. It runs once, the first time you create a workspace.

## The six steps

1. **Create account.** Sign up with your email and a password, or continue with Google. You agree to the terms here.
2. **Verify email.** Open the link we send you. Setup picks up where you left off, and the screen moves on by itself once you verify, even if you open the link in another browser.
3. **Your business.** Tell us what you want to teach first, how you work, and how customers learn today. You can paste your website, describe your business instead, or skip this part. If you share a website we use it, together with your answers, to draft your sample courses and suggest a brand colour.
4. **Your academy.** Confirm your name, your organization name, and your academy address (the subdomain customers will see).
5. **Customise.** Preview your academy as you choose a brand colour and a landing page theme. Some themes need a paid plan; you can still preview them.
6. **Build your first course.** Describe the course you want and the AI assistant drafts it. Everything stays a draft until you publish.

If AI is not configured on your installation, the last step is skipped and sample courses are built in instead.

## Sample courses

Setup adds up to four sample courses, each with three sections and nine lessons, so your workspace is never empty. They are unpublished and marked **Sample** on the Courses page. Edit, publish or delete them whenever you like. You can rename or remove them on the Customise step before they are created.

## Change things later

- **Brand colour and theme:** Settings, then the landing page and organization pages.
- **Academy address:** Settings, then Domains.
- **Sample courses:** open any course from the Courses page.

## If something looks wrong

- **The verification email did not arrive.** Use **Resend** on the verify screen, or **Use a different email** if you mistyped it.
- **Setup took too long to prepare your courses.** You can continue after a minute. Courses that were not ready are filled with sample content.
- **You left part-way.** Sign in again and setup resumes on the step you reached, with your answers saved.
