---
title: Connect from Claude or ChatGPT
description: Add ClassroomIO to an AI app as a custom connector and approve access with your ClassroomIO account.
---

AI apps that run in the cloud, such as Claude and ChatGPT, cannot start a local process. They connect to ClassroomIO over HTTPS and ask you to sign in and approve access, so you never paste a key.

## Before you start

- You must be an admin of the ClassroomIO workspace you want to connect.
- Your AI app must support custom connectors for MCP servers. The name and location of the setting depends on the app and its plan.

## Connect

1. Copy the URL `https://mcp.classroomio.com`. **Automation → MCP** also shows it, with these steps, until you connect your first app.
2. In your AI app, open the connectors or integrations settings and add a custom connector.
3. Paste the URL and start the connection.
4. Sign in to ClassroomIO if you are asked to, choose the workspace, and review the permissions.
5. Approve. The app can now use ClassroomIO tools.

For a self-hosted installation, the URL is your dashboard URL (`DASHBOARD_ORIGIN`) followed by `/mcp`. **Automation → MCP** shows it until you connect your first app.

### ChatGPT

In ChatGPT, open chatgpt.com/plugins, choose the plus button, and choose **Add custom MCP server**. Enter a name and description. Under **Connection**, choose the public endpoint option and enter `https://mcp.classroomio.com`. Configure authentication for OAuth, review the risk warning, choose **I understand and want to continue**, then choose **Create as a plugin**. OpenAI's documentation calls these plugins. Your account and workspace policies decide whether you can add one.

## What the app can do

The app can use every ClassroomIO MCP tool except `upload_video`, which needs a file on your own computer. Every action passes the same checks as the API: the permissions you approved, your role in the workspace, and your plan's limits.

Approval is tied to one workspace. To use another workspace, connect again and choose it.

## Remove access

Open **Automation → MCP → Connected apps** and remove the app. It stops working immediately. Access also ends if you stop being an admin of the workspace.
