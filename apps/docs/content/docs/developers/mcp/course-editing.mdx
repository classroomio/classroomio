---
title: Create and edit courses with MCP
description: Prompts you can give your connected client and what to expect before changes go live.
---

Once [your client is connected](/developers/mcp/setup), describe the course change in ordinary language. You do not need to choose a tool or prepare structured data.

## Create a course

Try a prompt like this:

> Create a beginner algebra course for adults with six short lessons and a practice exercise after every two lessons. Make a draft first. Show me the outline and learning goals, and wait for my approval before publishing.

Include your audience, topic, desired length, and any source material you want the client to use. You can also ask the client to build a course from a PDF it can access.

**What to expect:** The client prepares a course draft and shows you its structure and content for review. Ask for changes in the conversation. Publishing is a separate step; after you approve the draft, the client can publish it and give you the course link. If your client cannot read an attached PDF, provide its text or a file it can access.

## Copy an existing course

Try:

> Make a copy of Algebra Basics for the next cohort. Tell me whether the copy will be published and what happens to exercise due dates before you create it.

**What to expect:** An organization admin or tutor can copy a course's sections, lessons, and exercises, without its students. The copy keeps the original's published state, and exercise due dates are set to the time of copying. The person who created the MCP key becomes a tutor on the copy. Course templates cannot be copied this way.

## Revise an existing course

Try:

> Find my Algebra Basics course. Rewrite the first two lessons for beginners and show me the proposed changes before updating the live course. Keep the other lessons.

**What to expect:** The client reads the current course, prepares a draft of the changes, and lets you review it before applying it. Existing lessons and sections omitted from the draft are not automatically deleted. The client should start each new broad revision from the current course, since someone may have edited it since the previous draft.

## Make a small change

Try:

> Move the recap lesson to the end of Algebra Basics. Leave the lesson content unchanged.

**What to expect:** The client can make a focused change directly to the live course and report what changed. Changes like reordering content or editing one exercise may take effect immediately. If a request would delete content or student work, check [access and limits](/developers/mcp/access-and-limits#review-changes-before-they-go-live) before approving it.

## Choose featured courses

Try:

> Put Algebra Basics and Intro to Geometry first on our landing page, in that order. Show me the courses you found before changing the list.

**What to expect:** An organization admin can choose up to four published courses. The selected courses appear first, in the requested order, on the landing page and public course list. Other published courses follow, newest first. Each change replaces the previous featured list; asking for an empty list clears it.
