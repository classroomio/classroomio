---
title: How the Admin Dashboard Works
description: What each number on your organization dashboard means, how it is calculated, and how quickly it updates.
---

The **admin dashboard** is the first page you see after signing in as an admin or tutor. It is a snapshot of your organization: how many courses you have, how many students are enrolled, who recently earned a certificate, and which courses are furthest along.

Open it from **Dashboard** in the left sidebar, or go to `/org/<your-org>/dash`.

This page is an overview. For a single course’s students, grades, and progress, open that course instead.

## The three headline numbers

These cards sit at the top of the dashboard.

| Card | What it counts | What it does not count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Certificates issued** | Every certificate a student has earned on an **active** course | Certificates on deleted courses. The same student earning two course certificates counts as two |
| **Number of courses** | **Active** courses in this organization | Deleted courses. Draft or unpublished courses still count if they have not been deleted |
| **Total students** | Unique students enrolled in at least one **active** course | Tutors and admins. Organization audience members who are not in a course. The same person in several courses still counts as one |

:::info[Students vs audience]
**Total students** is course enrollments, not your full audience list. Someone you imported into Audience but never enrolled in a course will not appear here. See [How Course Enrollment Works](/guides/course-enrollment).
:::

## Top courses

This list shows up to **five** active courses, ranked by how much lesson progress students have logged.

Each row includes:

| Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Students** | How many students are enrolled in that course |
| **Completion** | Share of lessons finished across those students. If a course has 10 lessons and 2 students, there are 20 possible completions. Ten finished lessons show as 50% |
| **Certified** | Share of enrolled students who have earned a certificate for that course |

A few things this bar does **not** include:

- Exercises and quizzes — those affect course completion and certificates inside the course, but not this dashboard percentage
- Tutors taking the course
- Deleted courses

If every course has zero lesson completions, the list can look quiet even when enrollments are high. That is expected.

## Recent certifications

The five most recent students who earned a certificate on an active course, with the course name and when they earned it.

A name appears here only after the student meets that course’s certificate rules — not when they finish a single lesson. Configure those rules in [How to Create Certificates](/guides/certificates).

## Student login activity

The bar chart at the bottom answers: **which days of the week are students most active?**

It looks at the last **90 days**. Each student is counted at most once per day, so a student who logs in five times on Monday still adds one to Monday. The chart is a weekly pattern, not a raw login volume.

## How fresh the numbers are

The dashboard is a **snapshot**, not a live ticker. ClassroomIO keeps that snapshot for **10 minutes** so the page stays fast when several people open it.

Some changes skip the wait and refresh the snapshot right away:

| What you (or a student) just did | Dashboard updates |
| --- | --- |
| Create, delete, or change a course’s status | Immediately |
| Enroll or unenroll a student | Immediately |
| Change someone’s role to or from student | Immediately |
| A student earns a certificate | Immediately |
| Reset a student’s course progress | Immediately |
| A student marks a lesson complete | Within 10 minutes |
| Students log in | Login chart: within about a day |

That is why creating a course should bump **Number of courses** as soon as you return to the dashboard, while a lesson completion may take a few minutes to move the **Completion** bar.

:::tip[If a number looks wrong]
Refresh the page once. If you just created a course or enrolled someone, the headline cards should already match. If you are watching lesson completion, wait up to 10 minutes — one finished lesson often will not move a rounded percentage anyway.
:::

## Common questions

**Why is Total students smaller than my Audience list?**
Audience includes people you invited or imported who may not be in a course yet. The dashboard only counts students enrolled in an active course.

**Why did Number of courses not include a course I just deleted?**
Deleted courses are left out on purpose. Only active courses are counted.

**Why is Completion 0% when students are taking quizzes?**
The dashboard completion bar is lesson-based. Exercise progress shows inside the course, not on this overview.

**Why did a certificate not show up under Certificates issued?**
Certificates need to be enabled for your plan, and the student must meet that course’s rules (threshold, optional final exercise, optional deadline). Free-plan organizations do not issue certificates.
