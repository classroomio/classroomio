Unstyled show/hide region (base/collapsible, bits-ui Collapsible Root/Trigger/Content): trigger toggles the content, no built-in chrome; style the trigger yourself. Controlled via `open`/`onOpenChange` or uncontrolled via `defaultOpen`.
```jsx
<Collapsible trigger={<Button variant="ghost">Details</Button>}><p>Hidden until opened.</p></Collapsible>
```
