# CONTENT FUNDAMENTALS
> Source of truth for copy: Notion → Foundations (ICP and Personas, Positioning and Messaging, Website Messaging and Copy). Rules: no emoji, no exclamation marks, plain hyphens only (no em/en dashes), never "seamless/powerful/intelligent/robust/leverage/unlock". No customer names, logos, testimonials or outcome numbers until approved.

- **Voice:** confident, plain, a little dry. Short declaratives. "We are infrastructure." "Nobody sees our name." Talks to the buyer as **you/your** ("Your domain. Your brand. Your certificate."), refers to itself as **we**.
- **Rhythm:** triplets and fragments. Headlines often 2–4 word sentences stacked: "Learn Northwind. Get certified."
- **Casing:** Sentence case for headlines and buttons ("Start your academy", "Book a demo"). UPPERCASE only for Geist Mono eyebrows/labels ("CUSTOM BRANDING", "CASE STUDY", "NEW"). Wordmark is always lowercase **classroomio**; in prose it's **ClassroomIO**.
- **Specificity over hype:** concrete numbers and mechanics — "14-day free trial of the Growth plan. No credit card. No sales call." "Growth at 69/mo. No per-learner fees." "with one DNS record."
- **Proof via customers:** testimonials are short, outcome-focused, one sentence ("Our queue is noticeably calmer.").
- **AI framing:** practical, agentic — "Claude, set up onboarding training for this week’s new customers"; labels like `agent · drafting`.
- **Typography in copy:** curly quotes and apostrophes (’ “ ”), middot separators ("Open source · self-host or cloud", "5 courses · 3h · Certificate"), ellipsis character in placeholders ("Search paths, courses, webinars…"). British-leaning spelling appears ("colours", "favourite").
- **No emoji.** Unicode used sparingly as glyphs: ▶ (duration chips), ▾ (static dropdowns), ✓ (Verified), ★.
