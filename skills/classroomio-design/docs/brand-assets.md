# BRAND ASSET RULES (launch images, social posts, decks)
Distilled from Launch Gallery v2 — apply to any new marketing asset outside the website:
- **No box shadows.** Depth comes from flat colour blocks, hairline borders, or a photo bleeding under a panel — never `box-shadow`.
- **Square by default.** The only rounded shapes are `PersonCard`/`PersonFan` (30px radius, matches the landing page's photo cards) and small circular avatars/Stamp. Everything else — panels, book spines, rows, cards — is square or has the 3px "sketch" radius.
- **One idea per asset.** A single headline + a single supporting visual (a person, a flow, a book, a row list). Never stack three unrelated proof points in one frame.
- **People over screenshots.** Prefer a real portrait (PersonCard/PersonFan) to a UI mockup when the story is about a person being trained or certified.
- **Rows over boxed stat grids.** Use `DetailRows` for certificate/plan/setting facts instead of a grid of bordered boxes.
- **One blue element per asset**, used for the single most important fact (a number, a CTA, the Growth plan spine). Supporting elements stay paper/sand/ink.
- **Six schemes still apply** (`tokens/schemes.css`): pick one per asset, don't mix.
